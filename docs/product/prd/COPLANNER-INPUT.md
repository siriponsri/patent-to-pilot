## Five risks that change the release decision

| Risk | What would go wrong | Control and release consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Claims | A buyer interprets 66.85% as the chance that a selected drug launches, or that a low rank clears a case. | Print recall alongside 26.62% precision, with plain denominators. Mark H2 failed, H3 descriptive and L1e exploratory. Remove any implication of automatic clearance. Require matching wording on landing page, dossier, PDF and demo. |
| 2. Data rights | A public bundle or a commercial install republishes restricted rows, documents or derived fields. Public access and the repository's Apache licence do not settle third-party reuse rights. | Inventory each shipped file and underlying field source, redistribution and commercial-use terms, version and hash. Where terms restrict copies, provide only a lawful recipe or link. If an essential shipped field cannot be cleared, withhold it and disclose the resulting coverage or rebuild with rights-cleared inputs. |
| 3. Thai evidence | A family match or inaccessible register looks like evidence of absence. The six development keys do not occur in the current roster. | Show the denominators. In development, family leads existed for 17/17 US grants, Thai members were verified for 0/17, and both Thai patent status and Thai FDA registration were verified for 0/6 cases. Current dossiers retain their own unknowns. Assign a manual DIP or Thai FDA task per question. [EVP](../../loe-bench/receipts/EVP.md) |
| 4. Operation after January | Distribution, refresh or scoring depends on an owner's login, laptop or a scheduled GitHub job that stops when inactive. | Before 31 January, have a consenting maintainer restore the public version and run the fixture from the guide. Transfer release access if ongoing service is accepted. Otherwise publish a fixed reference edition and reproducible manual instructions with no update commitment. |
| 5. Trust in the interface | A pharmacist cannot distinguish development from prospective data, misses a stale edition, or takes a long PDF as evidence of completeness. | Label mode and cutoff on every page. At t0 the cited Orange Book edition is 10 July and 628/651 challenge histories remain unknown. Show the missing evidence and next action together. Flag critical wording or screen/PDF disagreement before distribution. |

## Ranked options after v1.0

Value and effort are planning judgments. The thresholds below are proposed go/stop rules to agree before a trial. Any ongoing service needs a named maintainer and resources independent of the owner by 31 January 2027.

| Rank and option | Value / effort | Success metric | Kill or narrow the option when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. v1.1, improve the one-page referral | High / low. Shorten the path from finding a case to locating its source and open Thai question. | After ethics approval, at least 8/10 evaluable C10 readers correctly identify the US endpoint, local unknown and responsible role unaided on a new development case. Report the actual denominator. | Any interpretation as legal clearance triggers redesign before expansion. Below 8/10 after one revision, drop the guided wording and retest a simpler summary. |
| 2. v1.1, local portfolio shortlist | High / medium. A target team maps a list of active ingredients to existing dossiers without uploading commercial plans. | On a declared public fixture of 50 names with known identities, correctly resolve at least 45, flag every ambiguous case for review and transmit no input. | Any silent wrong identity blocks automatic matching. | 3. v1.2, maintainer snapshot differences | Medium / low to medium. Show which edition, listings or tasks changed since a team's last review. | A successor reproduces two dated open-source diffs, reconciles all planted changes and preserves t0 hashes on both runs. | Without a successor or with any overwrite, retain manual dated exports and drop a hosted update promise. |
| 4. v1.2, paid onboarding pilot **(pricing hypothesis)** | Unproven / medium. Offer a four-week internal-install and hand-off pilot for THB 15,000 per team. This is a test price with no customer or willingness-to-pay evidence. | Of five qualified teams receiving the same written scope and quote, two accept a paid pilot and at least one completes a dossier hand-off.
