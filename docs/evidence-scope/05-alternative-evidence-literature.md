## Bounded research claim

A candidate domain-specific contribution is a representation and audit contract for deciding which version-bound, typed citation obligation to inspect. Alternative assignments retain their source, version, exact locator, inspected evidence, conditions and unresolved relationships. Conflicting conclusions can both remain valid under distinct scopes. An explicit version, applicability condition or claim branch can exclude one without treating the other text as false. The hypothesis is that exposing that structure to the next-opening decision improves a controlled scope task under matched access and reranking budgets.

The generic proposal to select a premise that discriminates alternatives is prior art. The literature does not establish priority for the narrower structured contribution, transfer, a journal claim or an expected positive result. A competing-premise selector and one-signal ablation are relevant controls alongside coverage. Treat the same-mechanism finding as a scope decision before expanding evaluation.

**Checkpoint:** original source overlap was sent to the Planner/Auditor before any expansion of the new evaluation. The Planner directed a narrower falsification target: when the canonical answer is identical, do different required witness bindings justify a different next opening, and does that improve complete supported success over a strong premise-directed adaptation with an identical final chain validator? An answer-only effect or equivalent decisions under the matched control would narrow the contribution to diagnostic evidence. S4 has independent authority. No fresh research evaluation score exists.
