# Thai health-law reading cards

Six local Workbench questions use three public legal sources. Each family has two readings of one immutable excerpt collection. Questions, Thai explanations, and clause labels were written for the project. These are public product examples disjoint from S1. They show how to read a particular source edition and do not determine anyone's individual rights, application status, legality of an advertisement, or immediate medical care.

| Family and reading | Verified original and specific scope |
|---|---|
| Medical devices · establishment registration | The original Medical Devices Act B.E. 2551, Gazette vol. 125, part 43 A, appeared on 5 March 2008. Section 2 provides commencement on the next day. Section 15 paragraph one, printed page 32 (PDF page 8), concerns registration of the establishment by a person intending to manufacture or import a medical device. Section 27 contains exclusions and conditions. The example makes no finding about a particular device or business. [Gazette reproduction](https://dep.kpo.go.th/law/raberb/other/supply-2551.pdf#page=8) |
| Medical devices · advertising | Printed page 48 (PDF page 24) contains section 56 and the opening sentence of section 57. Section 57 expressly subjects the prior advertising-permit requirement to section 56, which covers statutory prohibitions. A literal reference connects the two nodes. These cards read the 2008 edition. The FDA also lists the 2019 amendment, and provides separate current advertising guidance, including rules for professional audiences. [Gazette text](https://dep.kpo.go.th/law/raberb/other/supply-2551.pdf#page=24) · [FDA laws](https://medical.fda.moph.go.th/relevant-laws-and-standards/category/medical-device-act) · [FDA advertising guidance](https://medical.fda.moph.go.th/advertising/) |
| Medicines · claims advertised | Drug Act B.E. 2510, FDA-hosted Council of State consolidation. Printed and PDF page 33, section 88(2), forbids false or exaggerated claims about a medicine's effects in advertisements for sale. The selected clause does not establish whether the claims made for a real product are substantiated. [FDA Legal Division publication and linked PDF](https://laws.fda.moph.go.th/laws/drug-act-be-2510) |
| Medicines · advertising in print | On the same page section 88 bis names the applicable media, including print, and its subparagraphs (1) and (2) require approval of advertising content and compliance with the licensing authority's conditions. The comparison uses the named print channel and makes no determination about a particular online campaign. The consolidated PDF was listed by FDA in 2023. Its publication page is dated 31 March and updated 11 April. Neither date is recorded as the commencement of these sections. [FDA edition](https://laws.fda.moph.go.th/laws/drug-act-be-2510) |
| National health · individual health information | National Health Act B.E. 2550, original Gazette vol. 124, part 16 A, 19 March 2007. Section 2 gives commencement on the next day. Section 7 on printed and PDF page 3 concerns health information, disclosure likely to cause harm, the direct wishes of the person and specific legal obligations to disclose. The card does not offer access to actual records. [Gazette reproduction](https://www.cdd.go.th/file/get/file/1.2024120723a1fc47f8c742b95112e971fcbe212e000713.pdf#page=3) |
| National health · informed choice and emergency scope | On the same page section 8 paragraph one addresses sufficient information for a patient's decision. Paragraph three (1) describes a life-threatening situation requiring urgent assistance. Paragraph three (2) is another exception with different conditions. This card illustrates the first exception only. [Gazette reproduction](https://www.cdd.go.th/file/get/file/1.2024120723a1fc47f8c742b95112e971fcbe212e000713.pdf#page=3) |

For each record, the quotation and export decisions apply only to the Thai statutory language under section 7(2) of the Thai Copyright Act. The local public package may redistribute those excerpts with source attribution and the project-authored glosses. Website content, Gazette and Council of State page images, the agency marks and the full PDFs are omitted from the distributed product. No third-party weights, paid APIs or installation were used.
