**Source** `ae-th-bueng-kan-act-doc`, version `BE2554-RG128-18A`. Parliament of Thailand, *Act Establishing Changwat Bueng Kan, B.E. 2554*, Royal Gazette volume 128, part 18 ก, 22 March 2011, printed pages 1 and 2. The [Senate Secretariat PDF](https://www.senate.go.th/assets/portals/93/fileups/257/files/rk/47_62/2555/125.pdf#page=8) contains facsimiles of the promulgated pages as PDF pages 8–9, counting from one. The PDF is 19,223,930 bytes with SHA-256 `f90fee454a118b25d7bbd00bdde5f98ba3473b7c4da81ec6ff264f1eb16cd785`. The direct Gazette archive path returned HTTP 403. The author visually checked the six included sections against the Thai Senate's facsimile of the promulgated act. The scan's OCR text layer has extraction errors so the packet uses a transcription checked against the rendered pages.

**Reuse basis** Section 7(2) of Thailand's Copyright Act excludes laws from copyright protection under that Act. This permits reproduction of the statutory text included here. The package includes selected operative provisions with ordinary line wrapping and whitespace regularized and standard Unicode Thai vowel forms. The scan, page mastheads, seals, editorial discussion and draft texts are outside this extract. See the [Department of Intellectual Property text of the Copyright Act, §7(2), printed page 5](https://www.ipthailand.go.th/images/3534/2565/Copyright/Copyright_Act_TH_2537-2565.pdf#page=5).

**Scope** Section 3 identifies the district. Sections 5 and 7 each incorporate the list of districts under Section 3, but specify distinct transferring and receiving entities. The annotation key `district_seka` captures that named district from the list and preserves its literal value. The narrower phrases about the annual expenditure budget and the changing recipient come from Section 7 itself. Section 4 supplies the rename without a linked provision. The amount question is left unadjudicated on the selected passages.
