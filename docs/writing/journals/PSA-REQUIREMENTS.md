# Pharmaceutical Sciences Asia requirements

Checked 28 November 2026 ICT. Use **Original Article** for the full empirical study, in **English only**. Mahidol lists “Clinical Pharmacy and Social Administrative Pharmacy” in scope. The corresponding author submits through the [ThaiJO submissions portal][submit], linked as “Submit online” in the [current guide][guide]. Mahidol's older page points to Editorial Manager. The ThaiJO editorial policy confirms double-blind review. The ThaiJO page supplies the current submission checklist. [Mahidol scope][scope] · [Older author instructions][legacy] · [ThaiJO editorial policy][editorial].

## Manuscript and files

| Item | Draft to this specification |
| --- | --- |
| Length | Main text ≤4,000 words excluding abstract and references, with ≤40 references. These limits are specified in the editable template under Introduction and References. **MISSING — ask owner (PSA-01)** for any separate manuscript page limit or maximum figure/table count. [Manuscript template][ms] |
| Abstract and keywords | One unstructured paragraph, ≤250 words. Add 3–5 indexing words or phrases, capitalizing the first word of each and separating them with semicolons. [Author guide, Article Types][guide] · [Manuscript template][ms] |
| Structure | Title, abstract, keywords, numbered Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions, and Acknowledgements, then References. The template permits combined Results and Discussion. Use the Word template, double-spaced 12 pt Times New Roman, with page and line numbers. Main headings are bold uppercase in the template. [Author guide, Manuscript Submission][guide] · [Manuscript template][ms] |
| References | Vancouver. Superscript in order of first citation, before the full stop as shown in the template. List all authors through six and the first six plus et al. above six. Example rendered from the BMJ record: Collins GS, Moons KGM, Dhiman P, Riley RD, Beam AL, Van Calster B, et al. TRIPOD+AI statement: updated guidance for reporting clinical prediction models that use regression or machine learning methods. BMJ. 2024;385:e078378. [Author guide, References][guide] · [Example source][tripod] |
| Figures and tables | Prepare numbered figure files, preferably JPG or PNG, ≥200 dpi and <2 MB each. Prepare a separate Figure legend file and separate numbered editable table files per the template. The ThaiJO checklist additionally instructs placement of tables and illustrations within the text. Prepare both in-text placement and separate files for the owner. **MISSING — ask owner (PSA-02)** to confirm upload placement, figure dimensions and colour requirements. [Manuscript template][ms] · [Submission Preparation Checklist][submit] |
| Title page | Separate file with centered bold 14 pt title, author full names in publication order, numbered institutional addresses and corresponding-author asterisk. Give the corresponding author's name, full postal address, phone and institutional email. Keep author names out of the main file for double-blind review. [Title page template][title] · [Author guide][guide] |
| Cover letter and form | Cover letter gives the “highlight or novelty” of the findings. Upload the signed Consent to publish and Copyright transfer Form with the manuscript. Obtain all authors' signatures. [Author guide, Manuscript Submission][guide] · [Consent and copyright form][form] |

## Declarations

- **Funding, interests and contributions.** The manuscript template has Author contribution, Funding and Conflict of interest fields under Acknowledgements. State each author's role, sources of funding and any relevant relationships. Use the template's “none to declare” language when accurate. The corresponding author confirms full data access and responsibility for the submission decision. [Manuscript template][ms] · [Publication Ethics, Duties of Authors][ethics].
- **Ethics and consent.** Use the template's Ethics approval field. For human or animal studies, state committee approval and code. Identifiable human material requires informed publication consent. For this approval-level public-record study, describe the source population and report the actual institutional determination if one exists. [Publication Ethics, Human or animal subjects][ethics].
- **Data availability and ORCID.** Editors may request original data and research notes. **MISSING — ask owner (PSA-03)** for a required data/code availability statement, location or deposit rule. **MISSING — ask owner (PSA-04)** for ORCID requirements for corresponding and co-authors. The public ethics page and title-page template establish the requirements above. [Publication Ethics][ethics] · [Title page template][title].
- **AI.** ThaiJO requires disclosure of generative AI writing assistance in the separate section titled “Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process” before References. Give tool, purpose, review and author responsibility. The guide allows language/readability assistance under human supervision and exempts basic spelling, grammar and reference formatting tools. Describe Qwen use in the research Methods where it affected the study. **MISSING — ask owner (PSA-05)** for any separate journal rule covering AI as a research method. [Author guide, AI Declaration][guide] · [Dated AI announcement][ann].
- **Reporting guideline.** **MISSING — ask owner (PSA-06)** whether an Original Article of this design requires a named checklist. The public guide specifies manuscript components. [Author guide, Article Types][guide].

## IS2 and OSF overlap

The ethics policy says PSA “will not accept articles which have been published (except in the form of an abstract)”. The signed form also covers “one with substantially similar content” and certifies that no study data “has been or will be published separately”. **MISSING — ask owner (PSA-07)** for a ruling on the exact KMITL IS2 short-paper version, its access and review status, and the public OSF registration and any attachments under this declaration. Obtain the actual IS2 version and compare its text, figures, tables and data to the proposed article before the form is signed. Disclose the registration DOI and any overlap in the cover letter. [Publication Ethics, Publication Decisions][ethics] · [Consent and copyright form][form] · [OSF registration][osf].

**MISSING — ask owner (PSA-08)** for a journal-issued cover-letter template if one is provided in the portal. The public guide lists the title page, manuscript and consent form as downloadable files and specifies cover-letter content in prose. [Author guide, Example of Required Files][guide].
