# Science & Technology Asia requirements

Checked 28 November 2026 ICT. Submit as a **Research Article** in **English only**. The journal profile says “STA accepts submissions in English”. Use the journal's [ThaiJO submissions portal][submit] and public Submission Preparation Checklist. The [Author Guidelines][guide] link a newer editable 2020 Word template and LaTeX source. The submission checklist also links a legacy Word file, a Word-template PDF and the LaTeX package and its PDF. For Word, the checklist calls for both Word and PDF files. [Official ThaiJO profile, Types of Articles][profile] · [Author Guidelines][guide] · [Submission Preparation Checklist][submit].

## Manuscript and files

| Item | Draft to this specification |
| --- | --- |
| Limits | Abstract ≤250 words. **MISSING — ask owner (STA-01)** for research article main-text word limit, maximum pages, and maximum references, tables and figures. The example pagination is part of the template. [Author Guidelines, Manuscript Components][guide] · [2020 Word template][docx] |
| Abstract | Use one unstructured, justified paragraph under the uppercase “ABSTRACT” heading. The Word template calls for 11 pt Times New Roman and a self-contained summary without citations. [2020 Word template, p. 1][docx] |
| Keywords | Give 3–5 below the abstract. Set them in alphabetical order and separate them with semicolons as shown in the template. [Author Guidelines, Manuscript Components][guide] · [2020 Word template, p. 1][docx] |
| Structure and layout | Follow the Word template's Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results and Discussion, Conclusion, Acknowledgements, References and optional Appendix. Use B5, 2 cm top/bottom margins and 1.5 cm side margins. Use one column for front matter and two 6.95 cm columns with a 0.8 cm gap for the body. Use Times New Roman, 11 pt body and bold 12 pt main headings in the Word template. [Author Guidelines, Text Format][guide] · [2020 Word template][docx] |
| References | Vancouver in order of first citation, with bracketed numbers such as `[1]`. The Word template gives all names for fewer than six authors and permits et al. from six. Example as rendered in the guide: [1] Karlsson KG. Development of an operational cloud classification model. Int J Remote Sensing 1989;10:687-93. [Author Guidelines, Acknowledgments and References][guide] · [Word template reference section][docx] · [Linked Chulalongkorn Vancouver guide][vanc] · [Imperial library's current guide][imperial] |
| Figures and tables | Embed at the relevant point. Use Fig. in text, captions below figures (10 pt in Word), titles above tables (11 pt), notes below (8 pt). Wide figures and tables can span both columns. Insert clear, high-resolution originals. **MISSING — ask owner (STA-02)** for numerical dpi, accepted image formats, maximum dimensions or file size, and colour rules. [Author Guidelines, Line Art Figures and Photographs][guide] · [2020 Word template][docx] |
| Title and anonymization | Center a concise title. The publication template shows author names, numbered department/institution addresses with postcode and country, and an asterisk and email for the corresponding author. The author guide specifies double-blind review and omits names and affiliations from the review manuscript. **MISSING — ask owner (STA-03)** for a separate title-page file or title-page template and what identification belongs in portal metadata. [Author Guidelines, review and Text Format][guide] · [2020 Word template, p. 1][docx] |

## Declarations and cover letter

- **Funding and interests.** Disclose all sources of financial support and relationships that could influence results or interpretation. The journal gives employment, consulting, patent filings and grants as examples. The template includes Acknowledgements. [Publication Ethics, Disclosure and Conflicts of Interest][ethics] · [2020 Word template][docx].
- **Ethics.** The journal requires compliance with applicable laws and institutional guidance for human or animal studies, and an informed-consent statement for human experiments or trials. Describe the public regulatory evidence actually used. **MISSING — ask owner (STA-04)** for any required IRB identifier or non-human-subject statement, and the placement of the declaration. [Publication Ethics, Hazards and Human or Animal Subjects][ethics].
- **Other declarations.** **MISSING — ask owner (STA-05)** for the journal's current data/code availability, AI disclosure, author-contribution and ORCID requirements. The checked public ethics and submission pages give funding, conflicts and conditional consent rules. [Publication Ethics][ethics] · [Submission Preparation Checklist][submit].
- **Cover letter and forms.** The checklist uses “Comments to the Editor” for an explanation of previous publication or concurrent consideration. The ethics page describes transfer of copyright. **MISSING — ask owner (STA-06)** for any mandatory cover letter, its contents, and a separate copyright or declaration form if supplied in the portal. [Submission Preparation Checklist][submit] · [Publication Ethics, Copyright and Access][ethics].
- **Reporting guideline.** **MISSING — ask owner (STA-07)** whether a named research reporting checklist applies. The published manuscript guide covers layout and components. [Author Guidelines][guide].

## IS2 and OSF overlap

The checklist states “The submission has not been previously published, nor is it before another journal for consideration” and provides an explanation field. The ethics page says “Authors should not post their submitted work online” and specifies “prior to or during the submission process”. **MISSING — ask owner (STA-08)** for a ruling on the KMITL IS2 short paper and on the exact public contents of the OSF registration, including whether a protocol or forecast deposit is considered submitted work. Give the owner both artifacts, the review/access status and a concrete overlap summary for any explanation. The comments field provides a disclosure route. [Submission Preparation Checklist][submit] · [Publication Ethics, Copyright and Access][ethics] · [OSF registration][osf].
