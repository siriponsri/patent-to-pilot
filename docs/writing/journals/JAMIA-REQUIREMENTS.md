# JAMIA requirements

Checked 28 November 2026 ICT. Use **Research and Applications** in English for the original informatics methods and evaluation. Use the [JAMIA ScholarOne site][portal] linked as Submission Site. Prepare a double-spaced Word manuscript, or a compiled PDF accompanied by LaTeX source. OUP's supported-journals workbook lists JAMIA with the numbered, modern, large layout and unnumbered section headings. The OUP LaTeX package and essential submission checklist are available. [JAMIA Instructions, Article Types and Manuscript Format][guide] · [OUP manuscript preparation][oup] · [Supported journals][supported] · [LaTeX package][latex].

## Manuscript and files

| Item | Draft to this specification |
| --- | --- |
| Length | Up to 4,000 words, a structured abstract up to 250 words, ≤4 tables and ≤6 figures. References are unlimited. The article guidance excludes Acknowledgments and References from its limit. The title-page word count excludes title page, abstract, references, figures and tables. Additional tables/figures, data and source code may be online supplements. Tables over two pages go online as supplementary material. **MISSING — ask owner (JAMIA-01)** for a separate research article page cap and clarification if the two word-count instructions matter for a borderline manuscript. [JAMIA Instructions, Article Types and Tables][guide] |
| Structured abstract and text | Abstract headings in order are Objective, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion. Use the same sections in the body and add Background and Significance. Use title page, abstract without references, body with headings, inline Word tables, declarations, reference list and figure legends. Upload figure files separately and cite images and appendices in the text. [JAMIA Instructions, Research and Applications and Manuscript Format][guide] |
| Title page and keywords | Title. Corresponding author's full name, postal address, email and phone. For all co-authors give full name, department, institution, city, country and degree. Include the word count and up to five index terms, preferably MeSH. [JAMIA Instructions, Title Page][guide] |
| References | Journal-specific numbered, MEDLINE-abbreviated style. Bracketed in-text numbers follow punctuation with no space, for example `.[1]`. Order by first citation including tables and figures. List all authors through three, otherwise the first three plus et al. Italicize the abbreviated journal name. Example: 1 Collins GS, Moons KGM, Dhiman P, et al. TRIPOD+AI statement: updated guidance for reporting clinical prediction models that use regression or machine learning methods. *BMJ* 2024;385:e078378. [JAMIA Instructions, References][guide] · [Example source][tripod] |
| Figures | Upload separately, number by citation order, and put legends at manuscript end. Provide the required alt text directly under each caption. OUP's general artwork guide prefers TIFF raster and EPS, SVG or PDF vector with embedded fonts or paths. At intended print size it recommends ≥300 dpi for colour halftones, ≥600 for grayscale halftones, 600–900 for mixed halftone/line art and ≥1,200 for monochrome line art. RGB or CMYK is acceptable under that guidance. Size to expected column width using the journal PDF and preserve accessible contrast. **MISSING — ask owner (JAMIA-02)** for any numeric JAMIA-specific dimensions, upload size cap or separate print colour terms. [JAMIA Instructions, Figures][guide] · [OUP artwork guidance, p. 1][art] |
| Tables | Put editable Word tables in the body at first citation, numbered in order. Convert an Excel table to native Word content. Make tables self-contained, avoid duplication, and give meaning for special formatting. [JAMIA Instructions, Tables][guide] · [OUP manuscript preparation][oup] |

## Declarations and cover letter

- **Funding and interests.** Include named funders or an explicit no-funding statement, and all authors' competing interest information or a none statement. Give the funding information in submission metadata and manuscript. Include Acknowledgments and required declarations after the body. [JAMIA Instructions, Conflicts of Interest, Crossref Funding Data Registry and Manuscript Format][guide].
- **Ethics and data.** OUP's human/animal and privacy policies apply as relevant. State the actual institutional status for the study design and avoid claiming an IRB exemption without a determination. **MISSING — ask owner (JAMIA-03)** to confirm any journal-specific ethics wording or placement for a public regulatory-record study. A Data Availability Statement is required. State access and identifiers for all underlying data as applicable, cite public datasets with their DOI or accession, and ensure analyses can be repeated from archived data. JAMIA offers Dryad and accepts supplemental data and code. [OUP Publication Ethics, Promoting Ethical Research][ethics] · [JAMIA Instructions, Data Archiving][guide].
- **Contributions and ORCID.** Provide CRediT roles at submission and acknowledge non-authors' contributions. OUP encourages ORCID iDs during submission. **MISSING — ask owner (JAMIA-04)** for any stricter JAMIA-specific ORCID requirement enforced in ScholarOne. [JAMIA Instructions, CRediT][guide] · [OUP submission guide, ORCiD][oup].
- **AI.** Disclose in the cover letter and Methods or Acknowledgements, using Methods for integral research uses such as Qwen if reported as a method. Include tool name/version, purpose, and the authors' oversight and verification. OUP also calls for Acknowledgements disclosure of substantive manuscript assistance. The publisher exempts basic language checks, subject to the journal's requirements. Human authors retain responsibility. [JAMIA Instructions, Authorship][guide] · [OUP AI use and disclosure][ai].
- **Cover letter.** Tell the editor about related work, prior reviews and special considerations. Use the editors-only supplementary file designation for copies and correspondence intended for editors. Supply related published materials for reviewer overlap assessment using review-only appendices. Include the AI disclosures and prepare the short social-media summary requested by the guide for submission. If an NIH employee is an author, include the signed NIH Publishing Agreement and Manuscript Cover Sheet. **MISSING — ask owner (JAMIA-05)** for any general journal-issued cover-letter or declarations form and checklist beyond the OUP essential checks. [JAMIA Instructions, Cover Letter, NIH Employees and Social Media][guide] · [OUP Essential Checks][oup] · [NIH Cover Sheet][nih].

## Reporting guideline

The journal's public Research and Applications instructions specify study sections and do not name TRIPOD or TRIPOD+AI. **MISSING — ask owner (JAMIA-06)** whether ScholarOne or a current editorial instruction requires a reporting checklist for approval-level benchmarking. TRIPOD+AI addresses clinical prediction models, and its applicable transparency items can inform this study. The prediction unit here is a regulatory approval, so identify any adapted items and their limits. [JAMIA Instructions, Research and Applications][guide] · [TRIPOD+AI primary paper][tripod].

## IS2 and OSF overlap

JAMIA says authors may “make an Author’s Original Version (preprint) available” and that “this does not prevent submission to the journal”. On acceptance update preprint status and DOI per the guide. OUP screens for redundant and concurrent publication. For all article types JAMIA asks for related published material including proceedings so reviewers can assess overlap. Provide the exact IS2 version for review if related and published, disclose its status and new journal contribution in the cover letter and include the OSF registration DOI with the role and timing of its contents. **MISSING — ask owner (JAMIA-07)** to establish the exact KMITL paper's circulation, review and publication status and whether that version or the material in the registration needs an editorial overlap ruling. [JAMIA Instructions, Preprint Policy and Article Types][guide] · [OUP Publication Ethics, Concurrent or Redundant Submission][ethics] · [OSF registration][osf].
