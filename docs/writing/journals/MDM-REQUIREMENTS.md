# Medical Decision Making requirements

Checked 28 November 2026 ICT. Use **Original Research Article** in English for the decision study. Keep US or UK spelling consistent. Submit through [ScholarOne Manuscripts for MDM][portal]. Sage directs authors to the SMDM manuscript requirements, submission instructions and review process. Initial submissions are “format-free” with required components and anonymization. Revisions must conform to MDM style. [Manuscript Types, MDM Article Categories][types] · [Manuscript Requirements, Format and Style][req] · [Sage journal instructions][sage].

## Declarations and cover letter

- **Statements page.** Provide acknowledgments and writing assistance, ethics, consent to participate, consent for publication, competing interests, funding, data availability and AI disclosure. For human/animal studies report approval, waiver or exemption accurately, including board, institution and reference number as applicable. Use the guide's inapplicability wording for unused consent fields. Give all financial relationships or expressly declare none. [Manuscript Requirements, Statements and Declarations][req].
- **Funding and data.** Use the prescribed financial support and independence statement in both the cover letter and Statements and Declarations page when funded. The financial support policy also mentions a title-page footnote. Explain any limits on sponsor independence. The data availability statement must say whether and where the data, analytic code and study materials are available. SMDM prefers a trusted repository. [Manuscript Requirements, Funding Statement and Data Availability][req] · [Journal Policies, Financial Support][policy].
- **Authorship and ORCID.** Apply ICMJE authorship criteria, and acknowledge other significant contributors. Corresponding-author ORCID is mandatory. Co-authors are strongly encouraged to link theirs before acceptance. **MISSING — ask owner (MDM-04)** for a distinct mandatory contribution statement or CRediT field if one is enforced in ScholarOne. [Manuscript Types and Authorship][types] · [Manuscript Submission, ORCID IDs][submit].
- **AI.** The November 2026 policy requires disclosure of all GenAI or LLM research and manuscript uses and the affirmation “the authors affirm the accuracy of all outputs from Generative AI / LLM tools” in the AI disclosures section. It also provides a no-use statement. The authorship page calls for disclosure in the cover letter and the relevant section of the work, including Methods for study use. The policy restricts fabricated content, AI-generated data or prompt-built submissions, and other inappropriate uses. Document actual use, including the exploratory Qwen methods and attributable drafting or coding uses. **MISSING — ask owner (MDM-05)** for editorial interpretation of the rule on AI-generated data or submissions for this benchmark and its proposed writing workflow. [Journal Policies, Use of Artificial Intelligence][policy] · [Manuscript Types, AI-Assisted Technology][types].
- **Cover letter.** Required for submissions and revisions. List all previous or ongoing work using the same data, even with small overlap. Include applicable funding and AI disclosures. On revision attach a point-by-point response. The four-file requirements and submission instructions form the public checklist. **MISSING — ask owner (MDM-06)** for any journal-issued template, separate checklist or declaration/cover-letter form available through ScholarOne. [Manuscript Submission, Duplicate Publication Review and Online Submission Process][submit] · [Manuscript Requirements, Manuscript Components][req].

## Reporting guideline

MDM says it does “not require documentation of adherence to particular checklists”. The journal encourages STROBE where observational, STARD where diagnostic/prognostic and other design-specific resources. The independent validation policy applies to models intended as individual patient-level prediction tools. LOE-Bench forecasts at the regulatory approval level. Describe temporal evaluation and available validation precisely. TRIPOD+AI is a clinical prediction reporting reference. Applicable items can inform the methods write-up without claiming full checklist compliance. [Manuscript Requirements, Format and Style][req] · [Journal Policies, Validation of Prediction Models][policy] · [TRIPOD+AI primary paper][tripod].

## IS2 and OSF overlap

The duplicate-publication policy requires sole submission and work “not published, in press, or submitted elsewhere”. Its preprint exception applies to freely available manuscripts that “have never been peer reviewed”. A second exception covers unreviewed institutional or governmental working papers and technical briefs, usually with substantive revision. Disclose and cite the original, provide the original file for reviewers, get permission for reused figures and tables, and plan to update eligible originals with the eventual citation. Submit the OSF registration and relevant protocol materials as overlap evidence, with its DOI and the precise distinction between registration, forecasts and the journal results. **MISSING — ask owner (MDM-07)** to establish the actual IS2 circulation and review status and confirm which exception, if any, applies to the exact IS2 version and public OSF record. [Journal Policies, Duplicate Publication][policy] · [Manuscript Submission, Duplicate Publication Review][submit] · [OSF registration][osf].
