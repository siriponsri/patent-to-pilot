### Corrections (22 Nov 2026, owner note 22112026, "Corrections to carry into writing")

Struck text above is kept for the record; these notes govern.

- **§1, 25 November source check.** Paper 53 reports 257/692 exposed queries. The B53 value on 905 queries and IS1 post-hoc results on 725 queries remain separate. See the [source record](../../evidence-scope/00-organization.md#original-source-corrections).
- **§4, PatEmbed caveat.** The partial PatenTEB audit shows a shared family pool with public test files; training
  overlap is unmeasured. It does not establish that contamination raises this pool's recall, so the exposure ceiling
  cannot be called "conservative". Write: "PatEmbed's training overlap with DAPFAM is unknown; its direction of effect
  on this ceiling is unknown."
- **§6, A3.** The A3r0 panel is one Claude, two Qwen and one DeepSeek model, not four independent lineages, and its
  smoke calls are format evidence only. A3 tests one acquisition mechanism; its outcome does not decide all later
  acquisition work.
