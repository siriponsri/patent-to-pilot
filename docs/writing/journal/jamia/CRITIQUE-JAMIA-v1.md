# JAMIA v1 critique for the authors and v2 drafter

**Verdict: credible Research and Applications paper after major revision.** The publishable result is a temporally reconstructed US drug-review queue with a measurable review budget and a strong tabular comparator. An editor can assess the informatics contribution if a reader can reconstruct one queue and see precisely what the timestamp and linkage rules change. The present submission package promises that demonstration in a supplement. Two issues need immediate correction: the abstract's interpretation of the clearance bound and the cover letter's account of public dissemination.

Review basis: manuscript line numbers at `cd1748f1`, companion letter and declarations, [journal strategy](../JOURNAL-STRATEGY-4V.md), [IS2 English draft](../../is2/mwd/IS2-DRAFT-EN.md), [registered protocol](../../../loe-bench/PREREGISTRATION.md) and [JAMIA1 source map](../../../loe-bench/receipts/JAMIA1.md). Numerical verification used published aggregate receipts and estimates. The fitted models and protected row-level evaluations were not rerun.

## Section-by-section decisions

- **Title, objective and abstract (1, 13–31, 47).** Identify retrospective prediction of first final US generic approval and the intended reviewer. All five abstract headings are present. Lead with fixed-capacity event capture, followed by the negative graph increment. At line 23 specify that the *upper confidence limit* exceeded 3% at each horizon. The observed M3 rates were below 3%. At line 31 describe routine review as the protocol's fallback.
- **Background and Significance (39–43).** Set out the decision before the database problem. A later edition can contain a listing unavailable at the decision date. Describe how the admissibility rule deals with that case. Distinguish approval timing from market entry and time to first submission in the comparator paragraph.
- **Methods (53–100).** Add development cohort size and events (547 training rows, 25 and 46 events by horizon), five-fold whole-drug calibration, frozen age bins, shrinkage, feature transformations and ridge penalty. Give a versioned config and scoring command. Disclose threshold fallbacks at line 96, including the empty 24-month B1 clear set. Specify PCG64 seed 20261127 and linear quantiles. Explain the rationale for 20% capacity, the +10 pp reporting convention and ≥50-event-drug gate as three separate design choices. Date exposure, protocol amendments, gate and scoring. [Training receipt](../../../loe-bench/receipts/T1v3.md) · [Freeze](../../../loe-bench/receipts/FREEZE.md)
- **Results (77–84, 110–152).** Table 1 gives enrolled rosters but omits the eligibility flow. Include annual exclusions in the supplement. In Table 2 show captured events and absolute intervals alongside denominators. In Table 3 show observed missed-clear rates alongside bounds. State that queue weights permit fractional expected hits. Include grouped average precision promised at line 92 and calibration-bin denominators. Keep exploratory prompt results subordinate to H1/H2.
- **Discussion (156–164).** Use one workload example: 246 of 368 event rows in 924 M3 slots at 24 months, versus 241 for M1. Those five additional event rows explain why the negative incremental test matters. The threshold-based clear set differs from the complement of the fixed-capacity queue. Line 158 should attribute routine processing to the protocol. Discuss the absence of reviewer decisions, the anomalous 2024 evidence coverage and the limits of conditioning uncertainty on fixed fitted models.
- **Conclusion (166–168).** The main conclusion appropriately retains H2 and pending follow-up. Qualify “increased historical capture” with the cohort, comparator and 20% capacity. Reserve predictions of service benefit or automatic release for a deployment study.
