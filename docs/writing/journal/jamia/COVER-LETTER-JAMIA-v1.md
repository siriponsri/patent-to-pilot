The study evaluates a dated evidence pipeline and a capacity-constrained ranking for review ahead of first final US generic approvals. The pipeline records source availability and missingness and separates predictors from later outcome records. A graph-augmented evidence model improved historical recall relative to drug age, while the registered incremental comparison with a dated evidence table failed to establish a graph benefit. A separate clearance policy failed the prespecified safety criterion. We report these results alongside the provenance and evaluation procedures that produced them.
