## Decision for the next release

Prioritize the Thai pre-submission question when the frozen eligibility and ordering rules admit it. It has direct local practitioner evidence and an identifiable incumbent. Retain patent-record handoff as a separate comparator-oriented research direction. Establish evidence of a Thai user's recurring task before prioritizing it over the local RA problem. Keep biologics discovery and market coverage in the approved sequence.

For each deep dive, reconstruct the operator's starting information, pending decision and the smallest source-supported change in action. Report differences in action and unsupported escalations on equal inputs, including ties. A published audit could establish where public records cease to answer a decision across a defined sample. It would require a reproducible frame and distinct cases before any generalization. Purchaser demand, operator time and the benefit of a new workflow remain unmeasured.

Use these questions prospectively at the next Planner release. Preserve the original lock and record any amendment before the affected screen. No named leads selected in this review.
