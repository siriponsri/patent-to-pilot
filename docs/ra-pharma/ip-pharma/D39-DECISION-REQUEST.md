## Rules for continuing without another vote

Each batch starts with a question, intended decision, comparator and source budget, then collects, checks and publishes its receipt. Planner verifies the source locator behind each promoted claim and records accept, correct or park before releasing the next batch. Resume a failed batch from its last verified receipt. A tie retains the incumbent. A blocked lead can be replaced within the same track and cap; record the reason and original outcome. If no credible lead survives, release the negative portfolio handback early. Do not manufacture batches or rerun D38 on cosmetic changes.

One attributable public source is enough to *list a lead*. A missing hash on a permitted view-only source is marked unverified with edition, locator and access date. Only verified evidence supports promotion. A single hospital plan can support an institutional procurement lead with its period and units. National share or realized spend requires a compatible denominator and transaction evidence. A missing patent-family map does not stop D36 discovery. Historical independence and legal FTO remain unresolved. Partial records, null results and market-specific unknowns stay visible.

For Product impact record who would act, the manual alternative and the observable difference on the same inputs. No simulated time saving or demand claims. Equalize available sources, retrieval allowance and tools for paired comparisons; record actual requests, retries, spend and unknown labor by arm.
