# IP Pharma Agentic FTO: results for the eight owner ideas

**Owner handback · 26 November 2026, Bangkok.** The portfolio has a replayable pharmaceutical patent dossier, a claim dependency example and a Thai internal request for missing evidence. Two simple rules earned a place in the product: bind a US listing to the exact FDA product, and follow each explicit claim reference back to its root. The adaptive architecture has **no fresh comparative score**. Three cases were selected prospectively. None had both source options verified for the exact product edition, so neither policy ran. The evidence supports a portfolio demonstration today. It does not establish infringement, freedom to operate in Thailand, business demand, time savings or superiority over commercial assistants. ## A · Exposure-aware search

**Question.** Should an agent spend the next operation finding new candidates, reranking existing ones or obtaining a different source? A reranker can change the order only among candidates already present. In Paper 53's fixed pool, 70 of 692 queries had their first *known* out-of-domain relevant family in the top ten, 187 first found one at ranks 11–100 and 435 had none in the top 100. Thus 257/692 were exposed to at least one known target. Incomplete citation judgments leave other relevant families unobserved. These counts describe one recorded pool and one relevance definition. [Paper 53 provenance and project bridge](../../healthtech/audits/S3-20261125/BRIDGE.md) · [Dynamic Search Depth Allocation](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11486528/)

**What the new loop tested.** With a product and patent already identified, the proposed agent would choose one further source. Both arms would see the same source cards, have one additional open and share the verifier. The fixed checklist would open the label first for a marketed strength or form, or the quality review first for manufacture or core composition. The fresh intake failed before either arm received a usable pair. This leaves both the next-source choice and the broader expand-versus-rerank policy unmeasured. Agreement between two retrievers can be logged as a runtime clue; true exposure belongs on the evaluation side. [Execution contract](https://github.com/siriponsri/patent-to-pilot/blob/58855f6e13d1d35a6aad3307d014e08f0020af34/docs/ra-pharma/research/ip-pharma/NEXT-SOURCE-INTAKE.md) · [Patent Deep Research, DEXA](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-34374-1_20)

**Decision.** For a future study, choose one action problem at a time. For discovery, measure additional known families found at a fixed search budget. For a dossier, measure correctly supported missing statements at a fixed source-open budget. A new study needs fresh exact-edition pairs and independent judgments; the observed zero admissions yields no estimate of either benefit.

## B · Cross-domain query abstraction

**Question.** Does describing what a product component does, before searching, retrieve relevant families whose documents use different terminology? Paper 53 varied the instruction given to a reranker after the pool was fixed. The patent-aware minus generic contrast was +0.0008 strict OUT nDCG@100 (95% CI −0.0010 to +0.0028). The claim-focused contrast was −0.0007 (−0.0026 to +0.0012). Neither supported an improvement after multiplicity adjustment. Both estimates apply to that reranking configuration. No original-versus-rewritten retrieval run was conducted in this IP Pharma loop. [Project bridge](../../healthtech/audits/S3-20261125/BRIDGE.md) · [Patent QA and diverse retrieval](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2021.114910) · [PQAI retrieval implementation](https://github.com/pqaidevteam/pqai/blob/56342aaac5d9bf626f9413e5e49819e70709ce2f/core/index_selection.py)

**Decision.** Compare the original product description, a deterministic function/mechanism template and one model rewrite on identical indexes with equal query and candidate budgets. On a formulation claim, a rewrite could discover alternative terminology while dropping a decisive feature such as a material being on the surface. Score new known families and lost material features separately. Keep candidates found through expansion available for review; a successful search alone cannot populate a claim chart with a supported product fact.

## C · Claim-element FTO

**Question.** Can a reviewer recover everything a dependent claim incorporates and identify which limitations have product evidence? The ancestry pilot used the 45 numbered claims of **US11896586B2, Apixaban formulations**. Two had no parent and 43 explicitly referred to one earlier claim. A separate agent transcribed all direct parents. Reading a claim alone gave the complete ancestor set for **2/45**; including the direct parent gave **13/45**; walking to the root gave **45/45**. One-hop reading therefore omitted an ancestor in 32 claims. Claim 45 follows **45 → 44 → 39 → 33 → 30**. These observations are correlated within one exposed grant. A cached deterministic walk can achieve the same result. [Frozen claim protocol](CLAIM-PILOT.md) · [US grant](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11896586B2/en#claims) · [Patent Claim Mapper](https://github.com/mjtiv/patent-claim-mapper/tree/799010657392423ca0187b91910deb468c6f1b9c)

| Dossier question | Product evidence | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| What is supplied for ELIQUIS SPRINKLE? | NDA 220073 label, PDF pp. 7, 25 and 40; public quality review pp. 4 and 6 | Capsule presentation, labeled 0.15 mg per capsule and bead contents can be traced. |
| Does this establish the weight fraction in claim 30? | Label gives amount per capsule. The public review withholds the bead percentage. | Unknown. The measurements use different denominators. |
| Does this establish the specified layer and process? | Ingredients are named. The public manufacturing description contains redactions. | Unknown. Ingredient presence cannot establish distribution or that process. |
| Does the whole marketed product meet claim 45? | Several inherited limitations remain unresolved. | No whole-claim finding. |

**Decision.** Show the ancestry and a product witness or explicit gap for each limitation. The graph is suitable for inspection; counsel-level scope, independent expert agreement and cross-family performance remain unmeasured. PatentFinder's MolPatent-240 evaluates molecule–Markush membership, a separate comparison task. [Source-to-claim chart](https://github.com/siriponsri/patent-to-pilot/blob/58855f6e13d1d35a6aad3307d014e08f0020af34/docs/ra-pharma/research/ip-pharma/ELIQUIS-DOSSIER.md) · [PatentFinder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07819)

## D · Legal status and jurisdiction

**Question.** Can the dossier prevent a correct fact about one product or jurisdiction from being assigned to another? The frozen Orange Book pilot covered six ingredients, 34 distinct US NDA product keys and 85 listed product–patent pairs. Joining all products with the same ingredient added **48 extra pairs across 29 products**. Joining by application added **5 across 2 products**. Joining by application type, application number and product number gave **zero extra pairs**. All three retained all 85 source-listed pairs by construction. A second SQL implementation confirmed the overall counts. Exact matching agrees with the registry definition by design. [Frozen FDA protocol](FDA-PILOT.md) · [FDA field definitions](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/orange-book-data-files) · [NBER Orange Book study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10731339/)

The archived US listing for 11896586 belongs to ELIQUIS SPRINKLE 0.15 mg, N/220073/001. The ELIQUIS 2.5 mg tablet, N/202155/001, shares the ingredient and lacks that exact pair in the snapshot. The US grant also supplies an international family lead. The bounded WIPO, EPO and Thai DIP checks did not verify a Thai national member, effective Thai claim text or dated status. The internal Thai request asks for those records. An absent listing or an inaccessible register cannot establish an absence of rights. [Dossier and register attempts](https://github.com/siriponsri/patent-to-pilot/blob/58855f6e13d1d35a6aad3307d014e08f0020af34/docs/ra-pharma/research/ip-pharma/ELIQUIS-DOSSIER.md) · [WIPO FTO guide](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/pubdocs/en/wipo_pub_1062.pdf) · [Priorbit FTO workflow](https://github.com/Lawless-Inc/priorbit)

**Decision.** Give discovery, product listing, national member and current legal status separate fields. A country switch in the portfolio should preserve the sourced US fact and open the Thai evidence request. Only the US identity and listing part has been demonstrated.

## E · Funnel decomposition

**Question.** At which step did a case cease to support a result? Paper 53's exposed queries illustrate why counts help. Patent-aware changed the endpoint on 243/257 queries (127 gains, 116 losses); claim-focused changed it on 244/257 (123 gains, 121 losses). The near-zero means coexist with substantial movement in both directions. For this fixed-pool setting, the full mean difference equals the exposed fraction times the exposed mean difference when all unexposed differences are zero. In a nested multi-stage pipeline, compute each arm's success rate from its conditional stages, then subtract the end-to-end rates. Multiplying stage *differences* does not recover the end-to-end difference or identify a cause. [Paper 53 study context](../../healthtech/audits/S3-20261125/BRIDGE.md) · [PANORAMA decision-trail benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24774)

| Evidence funnel | What the stages showed |
|---|---|
| Earlier Thai correspondence follow-up | All three methods found the correct opinion on 7/7 adjudicated issues. All three scored 0/7 on complete required fragments. One additional issue was unjudged. Exact-fragment failure alone cannot establish a semantic omission in every answer. |
| Original fresh FDA intake | 3 products selected → 2 overview pages verified → 0 exact two-source packets → 0 cases scored. Preserve the third overview's HTTP 400. |
| Frozen same-case recovery | The same 3 product identities verified → 1 package-level quality lead → 0 pairs bound to the exact product edition → 0 cases scored. No substitution. |

**Decision.** Report access, identity, edition, sufficient evidence, issuance and correctness separately. Here the fresh study stopped at data admission. There were no comparative predictions from which to calculate accuracy, a tie or confidence intervals. [Thai inquiry audit](../THAI-INQUIRY-AUDIT.md) · [Fresh recovery receipt](https://github.com/siriponsri/patent-to-pilot/blob/58855f6e13d1d35a6aad3307d014e08f0020af34/docs/ra-pharma/research/ip-pharma/ACCESS-RECOVERY-RECEIPT.md)

## F · Validated reranker gating

**Question.** Once a useful family is in the candidate pool, should the system preserve the first-stage order or spend compute on a reranker? In the historical Paper 53 controls, Generic improved mean strict OUT nDCG@100 over no rerank by a reported **+0.0009**, with no supported improvement. BGE scored **−0.0080** against no rerank in that configuration. Those are fixed-arm comparisons. They do not identify beforehand which individual queries will benefit. No learned router, calibration set or matched IP Pharma routing result exists in this loop. [Historical study inventory](REPORT.md#disposition-of-all-eight-owner-proposals) · [PROARTSearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.09132) · [PQAI reranking controls](https://github.com/pqaidevteam/pqai/blob/56342aaac5d9bf626f9413e5e49819e70709ce2f/core/reranking.py)

Paper 53's secondary contrasts give a 95% interval of −0.0035 to +0.0051 for Generic versus no rerank (Holm-adjusted p = 1.000). For BGE versus no rerank the interval was −0.0136 to −0.0030 (adjusted p = 0.010). Generic versus BGE was +0.0090, interval +0.0052 to +0.0129 (adjusted p < 0.001). These historical aggregate differences justify retaining a strong first-stage baseline while leaving per-query router performance open. [Paper 53 source digest in project KM](knowledge-graph.json)

**Decision.** Preserve a no-rerank reference. A defensible router study would compare always preserve, always rerank and the frozen router on the same disjoint families, ordered candidate pool and output depth. Report harmful rank moves, per-family gains/losses and compute along with average score. Do not select a gate using labels available only to the evaluator. The missing historical family-exclusion ledger still blocks the separate eight-cell successor evaluation. [Successor inventory](https://github.com/siriponsri/patent-to-pilot/blob/58855f6e13d1d35a6aad3307d014e08f0020af34/docs/ra-pharma/research/successor/README.md)

## G · Reproducible audit trail

**Question.** Can a reviewer reconstruct a published claim from the same bytes and locate a failed step? The Planner ran the Research dossier's published offline replay. It verified the pinned FDA archive, US grant, label and public quality review, the two exact product keys, the listed patent, the path to claim 30 and the cited product passages. For the fresh metadata recovery, the protocol commit preceded the first logged FDA response; all **seven files** matched the private manifest, with four issuer payload digests matching the earlier independent check. The initial intake rule was recorded locally before selection and published afterward. The recovery has the stronger prospective Git record. [Dossier replay](https://github.com/siriponsri/patent-to-pilot/blob/58855f6e13d1d35a6aad3307d014e08f0020af34/docs/ra-pharma/research/ip-pharma/ELIQUIS-DOSSIER.md#offline-replay) · [Planner audit](OVERNIGHT-REVIEW.md) · [Agent workflow precedent](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-34374-1_20)

**What a reader can inspect.** The shared graph records paper and repository provenance as well as typed public-case edges. A product *has an ingredient*, an FDA record *lists a patent*, and a numbered claim *refers to a parent*. Each relation supports a different statement. Earlier ChatGPT and NotebookLM chat evidence was retained privately. Both products recovered a known qualification on one exposed Thai opinion; the UI record has limits on backend-mode verification. No fresh IP Pharma head-to-head, few-shot comparison or reviewer-time study ran. [Shared KM graph](knowledge-graph.json) · [Commercial chat receipt](../OPTION-A-IMPORT.md) · [Existing competitor dependency tracking](https://github.com/Peter-McCann-Strain/chemical-patent-analysis/blob/e50f08614cb272ceff1bfffb1ffbf83fb087b376/api/src/api/services/monitor_conclusion_dependencies.py)

**Decision.** Keep the audit trail in the portfolio and retain failed retrievals. A hash authenticates bytes; it cannot judge the meaning of a passage, confer redistribution rights or establish the completeness of a legal search.

## H · An evaluable FTO benchmark

**Question.** What fact can an independent evaluator actually judge? PatentAgentBench already includes a court-derived FTO task and claim charting. A patent identified in litigation is a known reference for that matter. PatentMatch instead matches claims to prior-art passages; MolPatent-240 concerns molecular membership. An FDA product–patent listing supplies a registry fact. Substituting one label for another would change the meaning of the result. [PatentAgentBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35031) · [PatentMatch](https://hpi.de/en/database-group/projects/data-integration-projects/web-science/paar-patent-analysis-and-retrieval/patentmatch/) · [PatentFinder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07819)

| Fresh case | What was verified | Missing admission evidence |
|---|---|---|
| EMCITATE · N/220963/001 | Matching 350 mcg product, original label and letter metadata | A quality review bound to this product was unresolved on both permitted discovery routes. |
| LAMLIQUE · N/221109/001 | Matching 5 mg base/mL product, original label and letter metadata | Exact quality source unresolved. The application also lists another strength. |
| GOMEKLI · N/219379/002 | Matching 3 mg product; a combined original approval package advertises a quality review | The February 2025 package could not be bound to this product's July 2026 approval row. The package link alone does not settle coverage. |

The two passes concern the **same three matters**. During recovery, the Executor used three openFDA metadata requests, two index searches and one approval-package HTML request. There were zero HEAD requests, zero fresh PDF bodies and zero scored matters. No comparative model call, pod or GPU was used for this study. The comparator, one-open budget and intended independent adjudication are documented. They produced no scored benchmark or estimate of agent accuracy. [Frozen recovery protocol](https://github.com/siriponsri/patent-to-pilot/blob/58855f6e13d1d35a6aad3307d014e08f0020af34/docs/ra-pharma/research/ip-pharma/ACCESS-RECOVERY-PROTOCOL.md) · [Case-level receipt](https://github.com/siriponsri/patent-to-pilot/blob/58855f6e13d1d35a6aad3307d014e08f0020af34/docs/ra-pharma/research/ip-pharma/ACCESS-RECOVERY-RECEIPT.md)

**Decision.** Retain the cohort and its exclusions. Evidence that explicitly ties a quality review to the selected product edition could justify a new pre-score review. A different family-disjoint cohort would require its own prospective selection. Keep unknowns and ties, include the strongest fixed field-aware checklist, and count unsupported assertions as well as correctly completed statements.

## What the owner can present today

Open the US product row, inspect the patent listing and follow claim 45 back to claim 30. Show the label passage for the marketed amount, then show the unresolved core-level measurement. Finish with the Thai internal evidence request. This demonstrates the concrete value of keeping facts, dependency paths and missing evidence visible in a single dossier. Customer demand, independent legal accuracy, performance gains from adaptive routing and improvements over ChatGPT, NotebookLM or ordinary RAG remain open. The eight proposals do not require eight shipping features. **C, the US portion of D, and G** form the strongest demonstrated portfolio story. **E** explains the research outcome. **A, B and F** remain hypotheses, and **H** defines the evidence needed to test them.
