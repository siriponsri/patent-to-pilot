Chemical Patent Analysis already records conclusion dependencies by patent, jurisdiction and source, and routes changes for human reassessment. Its authors report 62 exact chemical-structure recoveries from 86 reference structures, and completion on 49 of 50 named therapeutics in its search exercise. Neither result measures recall of all relevant patents. Its model notices identify separate restrictions including MolDet and unresolved provenance for MolNexTR. Use its public evidence model and workflow as a competitor. Carry only independently cleared components into a commercial build. ## Additional literature discovery

Two SciSpace searches asked which studies measure pharmaceutical claim scope or FTO, and which patent-agent benchmarks provide evidence retrieval or claim labels. The first surfaced PatentAgent's pharmaceutical QA and image/structure modules, PatentFinder's molecule-to-Markush benchmark, Freunek and Bodmer's FTO search and the Fu case study. The second surfaced PatentMatch, PANORAMA and CLEF-IP passage retrieval. These represent different evidence tasks. Search coverage is bounded by the two queries, the supplied bibliography and direct-source follow-up. [SciSpace PatentAgent](https://scispace.com/papers/texttt-patentagent-intelligent-agent-for-automated-4xs0g4b7ji2b) · [SciSpace PatentFinder](https://scispace.com/papers/intelligent-system-for-automated-molecular-patent-5c8vrf6dkiaf) · [SciSpace PatentMatch](https://scispace.com/papers/patentmatch-a-dataset-for-matching-patent-claims-prior-art-1nx75e4j7n) · [SciSpace PANORAMA](https://scispace.com/papers/panorama-a-dataset-and-benchmarks-capturing-decision-trails-mxx8j8kv7ydn)

Closest external evaluation for the intended use is the FTO subset in PatentAgentBench. The authors derive product-to-reference-patent examples from court decisions. These are selected litigated matters, with known reference patents and jurisdiction. A missing patent is unjudged. For genuine version changes in a low-cost US pilot, the NBER Orange Book dataset offers historical product–patent listings. A changed listing can label a change in a listing statement. It cannot label a change in enforceability. [PatentAgentBench, pp. 6–8](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35031) · [NBER Orange Book user guide](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10731339/) · [WIPO FTO guide](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/pubdocs/en/wipo_pub_1062.pdf)
