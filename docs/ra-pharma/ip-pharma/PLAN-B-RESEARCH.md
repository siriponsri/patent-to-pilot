No cohort has been selected under this plan. All numbers below are proposed design choices or resource estimates unless identified as previous results. ## What the experiment would establish

An IP analyst knows the product key, US patent and relevant claim branch. One material feature still needs evidence. Given the product identity, source editions, document roles, headings and a one-document reading budget, **can a case-specific choice produce more complete, correctly cited dossier entries than a field-aware checklist?** The unit is one question about one exact product and one claim branch. A well-supported entry or a precise evidence request can feed an internal Thai memo. The result concerns source selection during FTO preparation. A national clearance conclusion would need its own authorities and qualified judgment. [PatentAgentBench, FTO and charting tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35031) · [Priorbit FTO workflow](https://github.com/Lawless-Inc/priorbit)

The exposed ELIQUIS example shows what a question should look like. The label supports 0.15 mg per capsule; the public review identifies beads but withholds their weight fraction. A good reader can cite the former and identify exactly what would establish the latter. This teaching example and all three previously screened drugs stay out of the new comparison. The earlier 0/3 was a failure to verify two eligible documents; there were no predictions to score. ## B1 · Find usable matters before building the agent

**Sampling change.** Start from older original FDA approval packages so that the original label and quality assessment can be tied to a common submission. The previous newest-product-first rule reached a 2025 review and a 2026 product without proof of applicability. Starting from the package intentionally narrows inference to public, document-rich US matters. Report how many products the rules omit. A missing public report remains unresolved. [Drugs@FDA coverage](https://open.fda.gov/apis/drug/drugsfda/) · [Orange Book key and listing fields](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/orange-book-data-files) · [Historical Orange Book research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10731339/)

1. **Publish the intake lock before the first case lookup.** Pin a dated FDA submission-metadata snapshot, the existing Orange Book archive and exact filtering code. Proposed frame: original US NDAs approved 1 January 2020 through 31 December 2024 for prescription oral small-molecule formulations with a listed US patent for the exact product. Define oral route and eligible dosage-form strings in the lock. Group by active moiety, NDA and patent-family overlap; keep related products together. Exclude the prior pilot panel, the three closed cases and known project/demo families. Log unresolved crosswalks and residual public-model training exposure.
2. **Choose a fixed prefix.** Sort by original approval date, application number and product number ascending. Scan the first 24 distinct eligible matter groups. Resolve order and duplicate rules before lookup. Preserve all rows, including access failures; never replace one because it is redacted, easy, difficult or yields agreement. If the frame snapshot cannot be pinned, return a source feasibility receipt before case selection.
3. **Prove the subject and edition.** Require the exact FDA product key, strength, route/form, submission/date, listed patent, grant version and family ID. Locate one label and one publicly accessible product-quality review, or an explicitly identifiable quality section of an integrated review. For multi-product packages require explicit coverage of the selected strength/form. An original-submission title alone cannot prove coverage of a later supplement. A custodian may inspect a cover page, index or scope heading for identity; log the pages and mark any accidentally exposed substantive content. Preserve redactions.
4. **Bound the effort.** Per matter allow one metadata query, one issuer package page, and one identity/availability check for each source. Record attempts, failures, hashes, format, license/terms, permitted local use, redistribution and cloud-import status. Keep applicant PDFs private pending review. Public availability can support a local inspection while external upload rights remain unresolved.
5. **Deliver the funnel.** Report scanned → exact product and grant → both source roles → exact edition → disjoint and locally usable, with one reason per exclusion. Proposed decision: fewer than 4 usable families ends this batch with a data report; 4–11 support descriptive engineering only; 12 or more justify requesting a blinded pilot. These are cost-control thresholds, not sample-size evidence for publication. No source-choice policy or scorer runs in B1. ## B2 · Define the questions and build an independent reference

The question writer sees product identity and numbered grant claims, never the two answer documents. For each matter, use the lowest-numbered eligible claim with a listed product-relevant feature under a predeclared feature taxonomy; take the first unresolved feature in textual claim order after expanding its explicit parents. Resolve ambiguous parent chains under a fixed unknown rule. The question must specify one product, one feature, the jurisdiction and the review date. Freeze questions before answer extraction. Keep feature-type counts; do not rebalance families using answerability or policy decisions. [PatentFinder and MolPatent-240](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07819) · [Prior claim pilot](CLAIM-PILOT.md)

Two reviewers independently read **both** complete sources and the claim context. Prefer a pharmaceutical regulatory/quality reviewer and a patent-claim reviewer, with bilingual review for Thai rendering. Each records the supported fact or explicit contradiction, necessary qualifiers, acceptable alternative passages, source/page locator, and whether label only, review only, either, both together or neither can resolve the task. An unresolved disagreement remains unjudged unless an independent adjudicator resolves it. Record agreement before reconciliation and reviewer minutes. The executor receives neither the key nor case-specific hints. [Sufficient Context](https://research.google/pubs/sufficient-context-a-new-lens-on-retrieval-augmented-generation-systems/) · [ALCE citation evaluation](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/)

**Solo route.** No external team is currently available. The owner can approve B1 and receive the complete feasibility result without recruiting anyone. If expert review is unavailable afterward, one separated reader plus source checks can support an exposed engineering pilot, with identity and citation checks only where independently verifiable. Identify an AI reader as such. Keep judgments needing expertise unjudged. A claim of expert-validated semantic performance and a confirmatory release wait for genuine human adjudication. No reviewer will be contacted or engaged on the owner's behalf under B1.

## B3 · Make one architecture change and test it fairly

Both arms share a deterministic FDA product join, cached claim ancestry, source cards, extraction method, final answer generator and citation verifier. The patent specialist supplies one unresolved limitation. The legal specialist supplies the allowed jurisdiction, issuer and edition. The Thai document specialist renders only the accepted assertion or the request for missing evidence after scoring. Keeping the renderer downstream lets us attribute an effect to the reading decision. Similar claim charts and evidence graphs already exist. [Chemical Patent Analysis](https://github.com/Peter-McCann-Strain/chemical-patent-analysis) · [Priorbit](https://github.com/Lawless-Inc/priorbit)

| Arm | What it may do | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed field-aware checklist | Pick the label for marketed amount/form; pick the quality review for core structure or manufacture. For mixed questions use the first unresolved claim limitation. Break ties by eligible edition and source ID. | Primary comparator. Uses real domain knowledge. |
| Case-specific source selector | Choose from the same two sources using the question, feature, heading descriptors and coverage metadata. Proposed first variant uses a frozen feature-to-heading match and falls back to the checklist on ties. | Only the choice rule changes. Record both choices, their reasons and cost. |
| Mechanism check | Disable heading matching and route through the checklist with the same pipeline. | Confirms whether routing accounts for any difference. |
| Fixed-packet alternatives | Direct LLM zero-shot, fixed few-shot, full context plus checklist and standard hybrid RAG on the same permitted source versions. Actual ChatGPT and NotebookLM use separate clean sessions where import rights and access allow. | Secondary task of reading a supplied packet. Separate from the resource-matched one-open result. Record mode, citations, wall time, operator time and retained outputs. |

Every matched arm receives identical question bytes and metadata. Allow one document open, up to 16 MiB of artifact bytes, up to three cited passages and 45 seconds of local wall time per arm per matter. Build any shared descriptor index once and report its cost. Exclude snippets containing gold answers and evaluator-derived cues from cards. Use the same failure, timeout and abstention rules. Start with one policy change. Up to two further changes are permissible **on development families only**, one falsifiable hypothesis per change; freeze the chosen variant before confirmation. No held-case edits. [Patent Deep Research](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-34374-1_20) · [Earlier commercial comparison and limitations](../OPTION-A-IMPORT.md)

## B4 · Score, decide and hand back

**Primary endpoint.** For each independently adjudicated, one-open-answerable matter, award one point if the output has the correct product, edition, complete target fact with qualifiers, and at least one permitted supporting locator within the opened source. Accept equivalent wording and alternative valid passages. A correct source name alone earns zero. Compare the case-specific and fixed arms using the same denominator. Also report complete correct assertions per **all** adjudicated matters; retain the both-required and neither-sufficient strata. This makes the ceiling imposed by one open visible.

**Harms and costs.** Report unsupported issued assertions per all issued and all evaluated matters, incorrect country/edition, missed answerable matters, abstentions on both/insufficient sources, action disagreements, bytes/pages, inference calls and review time. A blanket abstention earns no primary successes. Distinguish an unsupported conclusion from a correct request for missing evidence. A contradiction needs a source that supports the contradiction; missing or redacted material remains unknown. Show paired win/tie/loss counts and family denominators. Keep a case-level audit with blind arm IDs through scoring. [Sufficient Context](https://research.google/pubs/sufficient-context-a-new-lens-on-retrieval-augmented-generation-systems/) · [PatentAgentBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35031)

| Observation | Predeclared decision to propose |
|---|---|
| No independent key or insufficient usable families | Publish feasibility or exposed engineering results only. Preserve all exclusions. |
| Same actions on every development matter, or all questions answerable equally from either source | Keep the checklist. Document why the study cannot discriminate. |
| More complete correct answers and no increase in the count of unsupported assertions on development matters | Candidate may advance to a **separate** prospective confirmation proposal. Show any paired regressions; a small pilot cannot establish safety or superiority. |
| Candidate ties, harms increase, or gains require greater source access | Keep the fixed method for the portfolio. Release the negative, null or budget result. |

For a 12-family pilot, a proposed allocation is 4 development families and 8 locked descriptive-evaluation families, assigned by a salted deterministic family-ID hash before labels. Keep surplus families according to a fixed rule, with no selection by outcome. With 8 evaluation families one discordant result moves the paired difference by 12.5 percentage points; a smaller answerable subset has still coarser resolution. For journal confirmation, estimate a credible discordance range from development, agree a smallest meaningful gain and an acceptable harm bound with the owner, calculate a separate sample-size target, and obtain new independent families. Use a paired analysis with family-level uncertainty and disclose public-data/model-exposure limitations. Keep the existing protected TEST closed. No journal tier or positive result is assured.
