# Where a professional decision could change

These are alternative starting hypotheses. Local practitioner burden, availability of a decision witness and strength of the incumbent should determine their ranking.

**Thai generic development.** A feasibility study interviewed five domestic pharmaceutical companies about modified-release development. Its regulatory discussion reports requests for more specific advice about clinical and nonclinical evidence. The sample establishes a concrete local concern; its national prevalence is unknown. A testable handoff is the question an RA pharmacist prepares for a pre-submission consultation: which uncertainty remains after applying the appropriate guidance, and which product-specific comparison would resolve it? Compare with an experienced pharmacist using the same guidance. Seek an independent witness for whether the question was actionable. [Primary study, parts 4–5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11541073/)

**Biologics development.** A CMC reviewer needs evidence attributable to the applicant and reference product. Lee et al. illustrate why an observed relationship depends on the product and range studied. D38 already tied a checklist on the elementary missing-report decision. Find a harder recurring decision only if a legitimate public record can establish what action was warranted. Ask whether the binding problem can be solved more simply by well-defined identifiers and human review. [Lee et al., Fig. 5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29469653/) · [FDA comparative analytical guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/159261/download)

**Thai biologics purchasing.** Nakornping Hospital item 1020440, infliximab, shows 200 vials planned for FY2569 at THB 577,800. Its FY2568 historical column shows six vials in a document printed before year end. NHSO reports 459 people in its FY2568 IBD/AS program context. These units, periods and populations cannot yield hospital utilization or market share. A buyer can already ask for the award and delivery record. Investigate whether a dated public award-to-delivery chain can resolve a consequential purchasing question at several institutions. If only plans exist, park realized-spend claims. [Hospital PDF p159, row 435](https://www.nkp-hospital.go.th/th/adminst/nafiles/2025-12-22-171735N.pdf#page=159) · [NHSO Table 2-14, row 31](https://media.nhso.go.th/assets/portals/1/files/รายงานประจำปีงบ2568.pdf#page=137)

**Patent and RA escalation.** In a US dispute the FTC traced one device patent across seven products, four applications and three ingredients or combinations. In a separate US audit, GAO counted 356 quality-related deficiencies among 435 remaining deficiencies in 26 generic applications. These are different tasks. For either one, ask which exact product, edition and responsible party would change the handoff. Establish Thai applicability independently. Compare with dated official records and a manual issue checklist. A counsel referral or a request to quality staff is an observable action; legal clearance and shorter approval cycles remain unmeasured. [FTC brief, patent 9,808,587](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/62-AMICUS-CURIAE-BRIEF-FILED.pdf) · [GAO-19-565](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-19-565.pdf)

**Cross-era alternative.** Could the early named-device source trail, patent candidate retrieval or edition-aware reader solve a present operator problem more cheaply than a new specialist agent? Name the exact handoff, show a case where the existing reader or search would be insufficient, and identify an independent outcome witness. An old dev gain or a replayable demonstration cannot establish benefit for a Thai operator.
