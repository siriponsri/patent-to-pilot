# Decision context | 27 November 2026

Patent-to-Pilot investigates when evidence about a drug, application, patent or procurement decision is sufficient for a specific professional action. Users of interest are Thai regulatory affairs (RA) pharmacists, formulation and biologics development leads, hospital buyers and patent-to-counsel handoff teams. The owner wants a publishable question with a visible practical consequence. Generic formulation, biosimilar development and the Thai biologics market are in scope. US precedents require explicit transfer assumptions. Patent-record triage is separate from legal freedom-to-operate evaluation.

The current decision is which operator problem, if any, warrants a new protocol. D39 permitted up to twelve public desk-research batches and closed after four under its approved early-stop rule. No lead qualified for deeper study. The manual checklist remains the default. There is no active Product implementation or established platform advantage. The owner permits a change of research direction and wants a local-model-compatible workflow. Earlier approvals do not release new experiments, recruitment, paid inference, GPU rental, Product integration or the held B2–B4 stages.

Source roles matter. A registration identifies a licensed presentation as recorded; it does not establish local use. A planned purchase identifies a buyer's intention; actual delivery needs a matched fulfillment record. A scientific article on one sponsor's biosimilar gives context for another sponsor's application. An exact quote can pass a source-integrity check while supporting the wrong product, edition or decision.
