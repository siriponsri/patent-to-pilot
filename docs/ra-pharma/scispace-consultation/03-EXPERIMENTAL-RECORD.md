# Results that constrain a successor study

| Task and material | Observed outcome | Implication for the next study |
|---|---|---|
| Reader routing, 11 authored cases | Learned routing scored 6 versus 2 for fixed specialists. All four net gains came from training-template echoes. On seven other authored cases each scored 2. Transfer and eight successor cells were NOT_RUN. | Separate training-like gains from new-case effects. |
| Patent claim dependencies and versioned handoff | On one patent, three methods recovered five paths with four distinct claim reads each. On six exposed handoff cases, exact composite-key lookup matched the specialist. | Use a cached exact-lookup incumbent wherever identifiers can solve the task. |
| Interrupted reading and Thai correspondence | Three reading strategies each completed 15/24 rows in 12 authored packets. On historical Thai correspondence adaptive and exact controls each completed 3/11 issues. On a second page all arms completed 0/7 judged inquiries; one further inquiry was unjudged. | Report paired regressions and absent judgments. Aggregate ties can conceal different errors. |
| FTO and B1/D37 | Earlier FTO intake selected three, admitted zero and scored zero. Original B1 stopped before selection because historical family and exposure coverage could not be verified. B1-ext NOT_RUN. Separate D37 feasibility found original labels in 24/24 groups and usable label/quality pairs in 22/24. A rights notice and ambiguous formulation account for the two exceptions. | Source availability does not validate scientific adequacy, independence or legal clearance. |
| D36 and D38 | Three discovery briefs led to a rituximab evidence-routing check. On two synthetic inventories, both forms requested the missing direct comparison and both routed the supplied report. Four fills by the same author; two ties, no operator timing. | A literature distractor and a new evidence row did not beat an equally informed checklist in this exercise. |

The biosimilar track stopped before enrollment: Purple Book reference names did not bind the reference BLA required by the frozen selector. No screened denominator exists. The Thai procurement screen examined eight visible-edition plan codes. All eight blocked at exact regulatory confirmation. The PDF omitted a printed page. Preserve the three distinct denominators.

The frozen queue and alternate list were empty. Batches 5–12 were NOT_RUN. Research recorded 65 logical accesses, 71 attempts and USD 0 incremental service charges; labor was unmeasured. Source failure limits what this screen says about unmet demand. No scored ChatGPT, NotebookLM or local-model run occurred.
