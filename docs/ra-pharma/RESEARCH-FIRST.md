## Candidate and contribution

Start with a Thai RA operator checking the conclusion and qualifications of a public inquiry. Derive the material conditions from the question and authorized sources. Record each condition, its supporting passage, edition, exact location and dependent draft sentence. A changed source invalidates the affected support links. The reader then chooses whether to inspect the old location, find alternative support, qualify the draft or request one specific missing item. A simple deterministic dependency tracker is a direct competitor. Measure whether adaptive re-reading adds anything to that tracker. Reusable skills can change only through a recorded development trial.

First compare current tools on one unchanged document packet. Test the revision mechanism only after admitting genuine old/new pairs with independently verifiable consequences. Include both substantive changes and changes that preserve meaning. Freeze acceptable alternative evidence and separate unclear cases from known missing information. If only artificial edits are available, deliver engineering evidence and postpone the natural-change claim. An assessment on Thai official opinions does not establish FTO, regulatory filing accuracy or business time savings.

Prior work already studies [sufficient context in RAG](https://research.google/pubs/sufficient-context-a-new-lens-on-retrieval-augmented-generation-systems/), [material versus immaterial legal changes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26530) and [coverage of regulatory conclusions](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44163-026-02305-w). [NitiBench](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1739/) provides a Thai legal QA reference and [NitiBench+](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03576) a temporal reference. Each measures a different task from preserving or repairing specific supported statements across dossier versions. Check novelty against these methods before release.

## Comparisons and decision

1. Compare the same base inference model in direct full-context zero-shot, fixed few-shot, conventional retrieve-then-generate and candidate configurations. Include zero-shot and the same fixed few-shot examples for the candidate. Give RAG a competitive retriever selected on development data and cite its configuration. Keep instructions, examples from disjoint families, corpus, answer allowance and budgets aligned for each matched contrast.
2. Compare the actual ChatGPT Pro and NotebookLM interfaces on the same shareable public source bytes and task. Allow good prompts, citations, full prior context for revisions and a common operator time allowance. Record availability, mode, prompt turns, imports and any unknown token or pricing data. A substitute API cannot establish product performance. Use private keys exclusively in scoring. [Notebook source and citation workflow](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16179559).
3. For document revisions add full re-analysis and a fixed dependency tracker with the same source links and cache. Remove targeted re-reading in a matched ablation. Separate product comparisons from mechanism attribution.
4. Proposed primary outcome is the fraction of adjudicated issues with the complete supported conclusion and all material qualifiers. Score equivalent paraphrases and alternative evidence against a prewritten key with system identities masked. For revisions score a pair correct only if both dispositions and cited support are correct. Predeclare which endpoint is primary for each cohort. Record harmful unsupported issuance, misses, false change alarms, abstention and coverage, document reads, operator correction time and observable costs. Time savings require timed operator trials. A single operator supports only a within-operator claim.
5. Before the first score on fresh cases, freeze the meaningful gain or acceptable quality loss, harm and coverage limits, budget, family-level paired uncertainty and the one confirmation candidate. Transfer to Product only after a reviewed gain in quality, or prespecified acceptable quality with a worthwhile cost or time saving. Otherwise preserve the strongest simple workflow. New capability, comparisons and transfer are NOT_RUN.

## Bounded autoresearch

Adapt the [autoresearch program](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch/blob/master/program.md) by keeping its fixed evaluator, equal trial budget, baseline first and one mutable method. Preserve every trial in a versioned ledger. Use forward commits and a candidate pointer to retire a failed method without rewriting history.

| Step | Responsible party and output |
|---|---|
| 1 · Find the actual failure | Planner uses SciSpace and publisher sources, checks comparable methods, versions, data rights and available adjudication. Owner critiques which errors would matter in a real review, using examples outside the confirmation set. |
| 2 · Set the contract | Planner proposes case families, split, key, fairness limits, meaningful improvement and permitted spend. Owner decides changes in endpoint or budget. Research receives only development data and a short experiment card. |
| 3 · Establish baselines | Executor records baseline quality and failure examples with one summary per arm. Give the tools an opportunity to solve the task. If no material deficit appears, return the evidence to Planner before building the candidate. |
| 4 · Iterate | Proposed first batch is at most three development variants, one hypothesis per trial. Keep evaluator and data fixed. Record revision, action changed, score, harms, cost and keep/discard/error in one row. Cache shared inputs. Return short receipts, retain full logs on disk. Stop on the batch cap, exhaustion of authorized resources or identical actions across variants. |
| 5 · Confirm once | Planner verifies that real source changes and the key can discriminate the proposed mechanism. Release the prospective lock for one new family-disjoint DEV capture only when eligible. Report paired results and uncertainty, including null and negative outcomes. Protected TEST and D31 pagination remain closed. |
| 6 · Hand back | Planner brings the measured trade-off and owner criticism to the Product decision. Until then, preserve the current Product branch. |

## Feasibility review and owner checkpoint

Planner verified the [feasibility brief](https://github.com/siriponsri/patent-to-pilot/blob/e5e8d61fe3eb9b0671add3179207fc29adff3765/docs/ra-pharma/research/FEASIBILITY.md) at the published research revision. For opinion 253/2540 in the first exposed pilot, the retained PDF and sealed trace matched their recorded digests. Lexical retrieval opened pages 1 through 3 and issued two page-2 spans. They omitted the page-4 route via a ministerial regulation. Both exact lookup and adaptive routing returned the same page-4 span and a page-2 span. This example establishes a material lexical omission and an effective simple control. The separate follow-up's 0/7 exact-fragment score cannot by itself establish seven semantic omissions.

The proposed first decision is whether checking an unchanged historical opinion is valuable to the owner. If selected, prepare a bounded feasibility comparison against full context plus checklist, hybrid RAG and the actual commercial tools. Permit a solo-operator engineering pilot with source-anchored rubrics. Independent human assessment, operator generalization, a cloud-import rights decision for each proposed file, and verified access to the actual products remain separate evidence gaps. Two reviewers plus a tie-breaker are a proposed publication design. No external review team is available today. Keep owner feedback separate from blinded reference scoring.
