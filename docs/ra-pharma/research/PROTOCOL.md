## Adaptive reading and evidence handoff

Every arm sees the same query, initial passages, candidate catalog, permitted actions, text and batch ordering. Candidate descriptors and edges must come from visible documents or pinned index metadata. The controller can deepen retrieval, open a claim parent, follow a legal reference, open an attachment or stop. A shared neutral tie rule uses a SHA-256 ordering of opaque candidate IDs. Include both ranked sequential reading and fixed specialist policy controls, and compare fixed to learned specialists with the same workflow, tools, limits and shared resource ledger. Compare learned with scope verification on and off on exactly the same reading trace. A single agent with the same tools and verification helps separate routing and organization in follow-up comparisons. In the v1 fixture, the single agent and fixed specialists share visible rank and source verification. The learned versus fixed specialist contrast changes route preference.

A source travels with role, subject, issuer, jurisdiction, version, hash, exact locator, trace and provenance. Admission checks compare the request to scope and authority. Where edition or issuer is unresolved, the output abstains or requests review. The consumer records dependency links to the issuing specialist's item. A copied patent value for a different subject is a harmful outgoing assertion even when its literal value matches the applicant result.

For natural reserved DEV, the primary comparison is learned versus fixed specialists. Cap both at the same completion coverage using the deterministic per-arm order of independent completion confidence followed by the opaque case hash, truncated at the lower observed completion count. Pin the completion-confidence computation in the run lock. If the retained count is zero, the matched-completion endpoint is undefined and the promotion gate stays NOT_RUN. Report full-cohort harms and harms on the intersection of completed requests alongside this analysis, with both denominators. Normalize the matched-completion difference in unsupported critical outgoing assertions by the retained count, report paired whole-family uncertainty on the intersection, and use cluster bootstrap with stratified joint resampling of families and reapplying the completion rule for the matched-count analysis. Keep unanswered, missing, uncertain and known-no-support cases visible. Secondary counts are correct completion, abstention, wrong-product attributions, harmful early stops, meaningful choices (two or more distinct available routes), cases without choice, initial baseline ceiling, and evidence/cost consumption. A result without independently supported truth stays uncertain. In particular, this protocol cannot infer freedom to operate from patent text.

Assemble connected groups using shared patent families, law edition families, or letter template families. Assign whole groups to training, development selection and reserved DEV. Independently verify no product demo family or known exposed family enters selection or reserved DEV; use only a rights-cleared and independently keyed reserved packet. Keep family-balanced allocation before scores. Freeze split membership, sampling and adjudication rules, effective dates, unknowns, conflict handling, input/output hashes, model, skill and harness versions, budgets and metrics before execution. Use training corrections exclusively on training examples and allow at most one promoted skill revision per freeze. Require improved independently judged correct completion on the separate selection split, zero additional unsupported critical claims or harmful stops, and shared budget conformance. Record rejected revisions and selection reads and time. No diagnostic-only result promotes a skill.

Require analytical budget ceilings and a feasibility estimate before admitting a natural run, including all candidate scans, full sorts and widths, actions, tokens, GPU/CPU time, training time, annotation and any money. Stop at resource cap or missing critical input, write partial counts and retain failures, with no substitution after observed results. Paid/API budget is USD 0 and GPU allocation is unavailable under this protocol. A numeric resource envelope and explicit release bound to exact commit, machine lock and output path are required before the single natural or depth benchmark original. Seal traces before separate scoring. Require independent review of that exact package and family/rights audit. A promising transfer requires at least five percentage points lower unsupported-assertion rate at matched completion, upper matched-count joint-family-bootstrap interval bound below zero, no additional harmful stops, no lower full completion and no budget breach. Report null and negative outcomes without promoting the revision.
