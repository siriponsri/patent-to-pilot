# Research executor brief

Use GPT 6.2 high in a separate checkout of `research/ra-pharma`. Start from the [board](../MASTER-PLAN.md), [contract](../EVIDENCE-CONTRACT.md) and [lane handoff](HANDOFF.md). The deliverable is reproducible evidence about reading depth, structural navigation and cross-agent evidence attribution in unfamiliar dossiers.

1. **R1 · Reconstruct the IS1 question.** Read the [source-page inventory](../../evidence-scope/00-organization.md#original-source-corrections) and [eight-cell bridge](../../healthtech/audits/S3-20261125/BRIDGE.md). Paper 53 found known cross-domain families exposed in 257/692 frozen top-100 pools. IS1's separate post-hoc cohort had 40.8% exposure at 100 and 77.2% at 1,000 (725 queries). These estimates have different inputs. Inventory permitted unexposed DEV families, known links, source rights, pool construction and overlap. Document which historical slots are reproducible. For a replacement retriever or cohort, rerun a matched reference and label the comparison as a new study.
2. **R2 · Freeze the experiment.** Compare 100/1,000 depth crossed with Generic, Patent-aware, Claim-focused and Scrambled instructions, subject to input eligibility. Add a controller choosing between deeper retrieval, a claim parent, a legal reference, an attachment or stopping. Freeze the primary contrast/endpoint, candidate rendering, tie rules, budgets (including full sorts), training cost, case sampling, uncertainty, stop rule and promotion rule before scoring. Record known OUT first-hit reading depth, no-hits, strict OUT nDCG@100 and actual reads. Report infeasible slots as NOT_RUN and give the dependency.
3. **R3 · Test the mechanism on development fixtures.** Compare a single agent with the same tools, three fixed-skill agents and three agents with learned skills. Ablate evidence-role checking under identical candidate sets, neutral ties and budgets. Correct skills on training examples only. Count meaningful choices, cases with no choice, baseline ceiling, missing evidence, wrong product attribution and harmful early stops. Use the [Option A diagnostic](../../evidence-scope/alternative-evidence/RESULTS.md) to check experimental sensitivity. Keep the natural denominator and distinguish hand-built challenges.
4. **R4 · Measure transfer.** Separate training, development selection and final eligible reserved DEV by patent family, law edition family and letter template. Exclude all product demo families and exposed sets from selection and reserved scoring. Build an independent evidence key where sources allow, mark uncertain judgments, and never access protected TEST. Pin code, skills, scorer, public provenance, budgets, input hashes and output path. Obtain independent pre-run audit for the one original. Seal traces before scoring. Measure unsupported critical outgoing assertions at matched completion; include abstention, coverage, per-family paired uncertainty and the cost of reading and training. Treat missing truth about FTO or legal practice as unknown.
5. **R5 · Hand back.** Report positive, null, negative and NOT_RUN cells faithfully, whether the learning rule transferred, individual regressions and exact reproduction commands. Keep research-only assets off the product branch. Update the lane handoff and push explicit paths. An auditable feasibility stop with named blockers is preferable to substituting a new cohort after seeing scores.

The initial target is the signed protocol, eligibility inventory and trace fixtures. Proceed to the frozen measurement when eligible and released. Compare with [SkillOpt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23904) on skill revision and [PANORAMA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24774) on patent examination evidence. Neither supplies Thai FTO truth. [NitiBench+](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03576) and [ThaiOCRBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04479) provide component context. Use only explicitly permitted non-TEST material. No generic multi-agent novelty claim follows from assembling the specialists.
