# Generic formulation | Rivaroxaban and enteral access

**US, 27 November 2026. PARK broad formulation development.** The intended operator is a US generic formulation portfolio lead, with a hospital anticoagulation pharmacist as the prospective workflow owner. Decide whether a new rivaroxaban presentation merits a feasibility slot for adults unable to swallow a whole 20 mg tablet. The documented alternative is Alembic's marketed tablet with labeled crushed oral or gastric administration. Xarelto also has a marketed suspension with gastric tube instructions. The labeled adult 20 mg workaround evaluated here is the crushed tablet. Local availability, indication-specific use and coverage remain site questions. [G1, G2](EVIDENCE.md)

Moore et al. compared a 20 mg whole oral tablet with crushed oral and nasogastric presentations in a healthy-adult crossover. The study enrolled 55 and Table 2 reports 44 in the base pharmacokinetic analysis. Crushed oral versus whole yielded 89.99% peak concentration (90% CI 86.08–94.08) and 95.28% total exposure AUC∞ (92.26–98.39). The gastric nasogastric comparison gave 82.04% peak (78.48–85.76) and 88.93% AUC∞ (86.12–91.84). The gastric route's lower peak confidence bound fell outside the study's 80–125% interval. This was a single-dose exposure comparison, with no patient outcome estimate and no post-pyloric treatment arm. [G3, Table 2](EVIDENCE.md)

**Strongest evidence on both sides.** Existing label-supported gastric options defeat the broad swallowing-gap premise. The label still avoids administration distal to the stomach. Its section 12.3 reports 29% lower AUC and 56% lower peak concentration for proximal small-intestinal release of granulate versus the tablet. The remaining post-pyloric issue concerns release site and exposure, so a liquid format alone gives no reason to expect it to solve the problem. [G1, G2](EVIDENCE.md)

**Smallest decision-changing check.** In one specified institution and adult indication, compare an existing permitted formulary or policy record against the labeled options and the local alternative anticoagulation pathway. Reopen a paper feasibility question only if it documents a repeatable post-pyloric-only or preparation/access constraint for which the current pathway fails and the portfolio lead identifies an addressable requirement. Otherwise keep parked. Current site pathways, frequency, implementation burden, comparative exposure evidence and a responsible reviewer are missing. The proposed user need remains unobserved. This check is a proposal for a separately authorized follow-up.
