# Biosimilar comparability | Rituximab applicant evidence

**US 351(k) planning, 27 November 2026. Advance one evidence-readiness question.** The intended operator is a biosimilar analytical CMC (chemistry, manufacturing and controls) lead preparing an internal pre-submission review with RA. Decide whether the planned dossier can substantiate a candidate-specific finding on the relationship between afucosylation and Fc-mediated activity, or whether the lead must first request applicant data. The current alternative is a manual FDA guidance checklist and source-by-source review of the proposed product's reports. Compare against US Rituxan. CT-P10 (Truxima, BLA 761088) is a worked example with a public measured comparison, and EU rituximab was a third arm in that example. [B1–B3](EVIDENCE.md)

**Measured evidence.** Celltrion authors compared CT-P10 with US and EU reference lots. The published abstract reports slightly higher total afucosylated glycans in CT-P10. Figure 5B reports no statistically significant relationship with FcγRIIIa binding and antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) within the tested drug-product range below 5% afucosylation. Figure 5A reports a strong relationship in an intentionally extended range up to 35%. Figure 3B also shows a higher-molecular-weight peak observed in the references and absent from the illustrated CT-P10 lot. These findings describe those materials, assays and ranges. The source is sponsor-authored and the admitted scope is the public abstract and figure captions. [B1](EVIDENCE.md)

**Strongest contrary evidence.** The association in the extended range and the observed differences show why “same glycan types” cannot resolve functional similarity. A null relationship in one narrow range supplies neither a general safe threshold nor acceptance criteria for a different applicant. FDA's September 2025 guidance calls for the sponsor's analysis of its own reference lots and a direct comparison to the US reference. A non-US comparator used to support the application needs a justified bridge. [B1, B2 §§ VI.A.1–3, VI.B–C](EVIDENCE.md)

**Missing applicant evidence.** Traceable US reference and proposed-product lots, qualified structural and function results with assay performance, and an attribute-specific risk rationale, comparison and disposition for observed differences. The wider package would need product-specific process, controls and stability evidence and an assessment of residual uncertainty for PK, immunogenicity, toxicity and any clinical evidence expected for that program. FDA can determine particular elements unnecessary. The desk review has no applicant result and confers no acceptance or approval. [B2 §§ II, V–VI](EVIDENCE.md)

**Smallest decision-changing check.** Propose one paper exercise for the CMC lead using a cleared or synthetic applicant attachment inventory, the FDA guidance and the public CT-P10 example. Compare a one-row evidence record against the existing manual checklist on an inventory missing US reference functional results, then on an inventory containing an attributable matched applicant/reference report. The row must change from a specific missing-data request to ready for scientific review only when the report binds the two products, lots, method, result and locator. If the manual checklist reaches both dispositions with equivalent traceability and no consequential omissions, keep the manual checklist. An unexplained discrepancy or any promotion of CT-P10 data to applicant evidence vetoes advancement. This is a proposed desk check pending separate release and an operator review.
