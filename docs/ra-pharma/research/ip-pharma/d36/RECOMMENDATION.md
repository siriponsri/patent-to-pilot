# D36 recommendation to Planner

**Take one biosimilar question to independent review.** For a US rituximab biosimilar CMC lead, decide whether a dossier row addressing afucosylation and Fc-mediated function can move to scientific review or needs an applicant evidence request first. CT-P10 versus US/EU rituximab supplies a bounded primary comparison. FDA's 2025 guidance supplies the documentation workflow. The comparator for a future desk check is the lead's manual guidance checklist and report review. No operator benefit has been measured. [Brief and prospective desk check](02-BIOSIMILAR-COMPARABILITY.md)

The strongest objection is that the manual checklist may already locate the gap, and CT-P10 omits the new applicant's lot data. The exercise is reversible: park the workflow improvement if the manual checklist makes both correct dispositions with equivalent traceability, or if the evidence record upgrades a finding without an attributable report. A difference under the proposed exercise could justify a later review of a narrow workflow question. It would carry no candidate or commercial decision.
