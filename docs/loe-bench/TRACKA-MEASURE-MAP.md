## Scope and inputs

- OSF `72-20` says “Track A repeats H1–H3 as a separate fixed-sequence family” and “Keep feature definitions, graph v1, model settings, calibration procedure and policy-selection rules fixed.” The general secondary obligations in `72-61` therefore apply to the calibrated Track A core where the fields exist. B1, M1 and M3 form that core. `72-20` says “B3 is a descriptive Track A expiry-only comparator.” [Prospective protocol, lines 90–101](PREREGISTRATION.md#track-a-prospective-protocol).
- `p` below means the frozen `calibrated` field for B1, M1 and M3. `native` and `diagnostic_action` refer to the exact checked prediction row in each arm's `arm-dictionary.json.files[horizon]`. An initial development-shaped package nests those fields under `arms[arm]`. A tagged release stores them at the row root. The package inventory is pinned by `release.json`, the freeze manifest and the external U4 digest. `Y` comes from a linked first final original FDA ANDA date in `(t0, maturity]`, with source snapshot digests and locators in `outcome-source-manifest.json` and `event-witnesses.json`. `key` is the ordered unique FIX2c key in `roster.jsonl`. [Release schema and checks](../../loe-bench/loe_bench/tracka_release.py) · [Endpoint and coverage](../../loe-bench/loe_bench/tracka_outcomes.py).
- Paths in the tables are under `results.json`. `P` stands for `panels.<horizon>`, `A` for `P.arms.<arm>`, `S` for `P.secondary`, and `D` for `S.arms.<arm>.diagnostic`. Track A has one cutoff per horizon. Formal H3 uses the first attested 12-month panel. Without it, the family remains descriptive.

## Dated limitations

- **28 November 2026 · Predictor missingness and time drift.** The public roster and predictions support row counts, outcomes, frozen action and queue checks. Longitudinal drift requires more than one t0, and per-predictor missingness requires the frozen predictor/evidence projection. The readout reports outcome-source coverage and identity checks and cites the [freeze-time coverage inventory](receipts/TRACKA-CORE.md). Predictor-level and cross-cutoff readout summaries are `NOT_ESTIMABLE` from this package.
- **28 November 2026 · Optional arms.** Where the public dictionary says `WITHHELD` or `NOT_RUN`, the corresponding arm level is absent and a paired contrast is `NOT_RUN`. The freeze verifies availability separately from redistribution rights. Under the U4 rules, B3 and P1/P2 supply native scores for their named measures. The calibrated-core policy and ECE measures require the core fields. [Release guide](HOW-TO-SCORE-TRACK-A.md).

For B1 and M1 the legacy policy answers when `max(p, 1-p)` reaches the fitted threshold, and predicts entry above 0.5. For M3 both branches predict no entry through `1 - threshold`. Above that value the conflict flag identifies the BLOCKED branch because the diagnostic high threshold is already reached. A blocked row predicts no entry below the legacy high threshold and refers otherwise. The unblocked row refers below the high threshold and predicts entry at or above it. Rank answered rows by the probability of the action taken, average exact confidence ties and subtract the correct-first oracle for eligible E-AURC. [Frozen writer](../../loe-bench/scripts/tracka_core.py) · [Legacy actions and metrics](../../loe-bench/loe_bench/k20t1.py) · [Graph branching](../../loe-bench/loe_bench/evidence_graph.py).

The separately versioned module `tracka_secondary` v`1.1.0` writes the secondary panel and draw keys prefixed `<horizon>m:secondary:`. Core paired secondary contrasts use the same drug order, 2,000 draws, seed and percentile rule as the primary scorer. The descriptive checks leave `tracka_statistics` and the registered primary inputs intact. [Prospective protocol](receipts/TRACKA-READOUT2-PROTOCOL.md).
