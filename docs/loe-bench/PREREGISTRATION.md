## Study Information

### Research questions and hypotheses

Does the graph-augmented model (M3) capture more upcoming first final ANDA approvals than the age-hazard baseline (B1) at 24 months? Does the graph add capture over the matched flat model (M1) at 24 months? Does M3 also improve capture over B1 at 12 months?

The criteria v3 endpoint is pooled event-row recall at a 20% per-cutoff review budget, using each arm's native score. Test these directional alternatives in this fixed order, with null differences at or below zero.

| --- | ---: | --- |
| H1 | 24 months | M3 recall − B1 recall > 0 |
| H2 | 24 months | M3 recall − M1 recall > 0 |
| H3 | 12 months | M3 recall − B1 recall > 0 |

Retain eligible extreme observations, with the prespecified age clipping inside B1. There is no statistical outlier deletion or sampling-weight adjustment. ## Analyses

### Statistical models

Fit each arm separately by horizon with the fixed TRAIN cutoff and identical eligibility. B1 is the native age-hazard table. Clip age to 0–30 years, use one-year bins, smooth toward TRAIN prevalence with prior strength 5 and use TRAIN prevalence for unseen bins. M1 uses age and age squared, NME, orphan and priority flags, oral and injectable indicators, prior NDA count and the clipped B1 logit. Add years remaining for NCE and other exclusivity, patent count, time to last listed patent expiry, time since earliest patent submission, challenge present/missing, distinct strengths and distinct form/route pairs, and the frozen missingness indicators. Use the ordered feature interface in the configuration. M3 adds one-hot OPEN, BLOCKED and CONTESTED indicators to the same M1 inputs.

Graph v1 uses Kleene three-valued logic for `exclusivity AND (patent OR challenge)`. Each expiry leaf asks whether all recorded dates fall within the horizon, with no dates coded UNKNOWN. TRUE, FALSE and UNKNOWN at the root map to OPEN, BLOCKED and CONTESTED. The challenge leaf is an evidence signal. M1 and M3 both use standardized L2 logistic regression (L2 = 100, 300 epochs, learning rate 0.3, zero initial coefficients and intercept, standardization floor 1e−9). Their native scores are uncalibrated fitted probabilities, with M3 scored before graph actions. B1's native score is its uncalibrated bin probability. [Frozen configuration](../../loe-bench/data/FROZEN_CONFIG.json) · [Graph implementation](../../loe-bench/loe_bench/evidence_graph.py)

For secondary probability summaries and policy, fit one sigmoid per arm/horizon to native logits from whole-drug out-of-fold TRAIN predictions at the latest TRAIN cutoff. Assign five folds by the first eight SHA-256 bytes of the UTF-8 drug key interpreted big-endian modulo five. Clip probabilities at epsilon 1e−8 before logit. Use L2 = 1 and the same iteration count, learning rate, initialization and scale floor as the logistic models. The final native fits use all eligible TRAIN rows and the sigmoid and policy are fixed from inner TRAIN. Exact fitted calibration and diagnostic policy settings are recorded in the configuration. Calibration can change rankings, so the primary queue uses native scores. [Frozen configuration](../../loe-bench/data/FROZEN_CONFIG.json) · [FREEZE](receipts/FREEZE.md)

At cutoff `t`, rank all eligible rows in descending native risk and allocate exactly floor(`N_t/5`) slots. Break a boundary tie by uniform sampling without replacement, independently across cutoffs. For analysis, credit each tied row with the fraction of the tied group that fits in the remaining slots. Divide the pooled expected event hits by all event rows. Queue precision divides the same numerator by all slots. Distinct event-drug capture averages, across event drugs, one minus the product of the miss probabilities at that drug's positive cutoffs. [T1v3](receipts/T1v3.md) · [FREEZE](receipts/FREEZE.md)

### Inference criteria and analysis failures

Use 2,000 paired whole-drug bootstrap draws per horizon with NumPy `default_rng` (PCG64), seed 20261127 reset per horizon. Sample the observed number of drug histories with replacement, using the same sampled histories across arms. Each sampled copy contributes all of its rows and counts separately for drug capture. Recompute the per-cutoff queue size, selection and fractional tie effects in every draw with model fits held fixed. Use the alpha percentile of the paired difference distribution as the one-sided lower bound. A step succeeds only when the bound strictly exceeds zero. Test H2 only after H1 succeeds, and H3 only after H2 succeeds. A failed or unevaluable step stops the confirmatory sequence. Report observed contrasts and two-sided 95% percentile intervals where defined, with unreached steps reported descriptively. [Field & Welsh, clustered bootstrap](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9868.2007.00593.x) · [FDA fixed-sequence guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/multiple-endpoints-clinical-trials) · [Frozen configuration](../../loe-bench/data/FROZEN_CONFIG.json)

Any undefined replicate makes that endpoint interval unevaluable. Retain the count of invalid draws and stop confirmation when it affects a tested step. Empty event denominators, nonfinite scores, missing required evidence digests and failed fit or queue checks are recorded with their exact cause. Do not drop or replace invalid draws, switch to another endpoint or choose an alternative model using test results. A metric with insufficient coverage is reported as undefined where required by its definition. Report failures of the gate or release checks as unexecuted confirmation.
