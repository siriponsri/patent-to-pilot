# E6 | FDA Paragraph IV certifications as of the cutoff

**Partial coverage.** Eleven of 16 annual cutoffs have a parsed FDA list dated within six calendar months and captured by the cutoff. The two current FDA downloads represent the same October 27, 2026 publication. Each contains 1,650 parsed rows. The current files are inventory only.

## Acquisition and decision rule

Use public FDA listing pages and Wayback CDX. Start with the current and legacy listing URLs, discover full-list downloads from archived links, and query both modern media paths and legacy PDF paths. Prioritize up to four distinct download payloads per July–January window, newest capture first. Fetch exact CDX locators at least 1.1 seconds apart in raw replay mode. Require an exact digest match and reject replay redirects. Use the FDA date in a download title or a current listing link. Where the PDF has no explicit title date, use its latest embedded creation or modification date as a conservative file-edition proxy. The capture of the exact bytes establishes availability. The historical FDA page tables list selected additions, so the page captures serve as download locators.

`PIVHistory.edition_asof` selects the latest parsed edition with edition date from cutoff minus six calendar months through cutoff, inclusive, and capture date no later than cutoff in UTC. The rule also excludes an edition dated after its own capture. Within the selected edition, `challenge_asof` returns TRUE for a uniquely linked row with a usable first-submission date on or before cutoff. A known ingredient with an unresolved dosage form, ambiguous key, blank or unusable date, or a future-only date stays UNKNOWN. Missing or unverifiable edition bytes also yield UNKNOWN.

**Absence assumption.** FALSE means the ingredient is absent under the matching policy in the fresh published list. FDA can delay publication or revision while issues are under review. A FALSE result therefore measures list absence under this join, with possible linkage errors and publication lag. Use a catalog cut off at the same date for regulatory identity and dosage form. A submission first revealed by a later edition never changes an earlier result. [FDA list description](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/abbreviated-new-drug-application-anda/patent-certifications-and-suitability-petitions)

### Entity linkage

Match normalized ingredient name and dosage form against products with a dated availability record. Preserve release and route qualifiers, then use a caller-supplied ingredient resolver when an exact name/form alias is absent. The default calls the legacy LOE-Bench ingredient normalizer. For FIX2, pass `resolve=lambda names: registry.composition(names).key` to `EntityMatcher` and provide product aliases keyed by the same moiety resolver. The synthetic tests cover the opaque FIX2 key shape. A real FIX2 join was NOT_RUN.

The audit denominator is every PIV row in the selected edition at each development cutoff with a fresh, timely PIV edition and Orange Book edition. The Orange Book catalog uses NDA products and explicit RLD flags for the headerless 2009 edition. It uses only products approved by the cutoff. ## Failures and limitations

- Several input PDFs encode merged rows or reference missing PDF content objects. This includes the October 2014 payloads for cutoff 2015. The manifest retains their bytes and parse errors. The parser admits recognized tagged source layouts. General PDF recovery and image OCR were outside this card.
- The 2012 and 2013 archive windows lacked fresh retrievable full-list bytes in the checked locators. The 2015 July replay of UCM293268 contains a June 24 edition, before the July 1 freshness floor. The November 2023 listing identifies a new PDF at a rolling URL. CDX supplied no matching timely download in the checked variants. [November 2023 listing](https://web.archive.org/web/20231130151743id_/https://www.fda.gov/drugs/abbreviated-new-drug-application-anda/patent-certifications-and-suitability-petitions)
- Initial CDX and replay attempts returned Windows connection refusals or resets. A later pass recovered relevant sources, including the December 2019 PDF. Some nominal HTML captures returned other payloads. Retries and failures remain in the manifest. The collection used standard public endpoints and raw Wayback replay.
- Legacy records can omit first-submission dates or use pre-MMA markers. Regulatory absence and matches can both be wrong when a source name or dosage form cannot be mapped. Unmatched names are the largest remaining source of false absence. The match-rate audit measures linkage coverage.
