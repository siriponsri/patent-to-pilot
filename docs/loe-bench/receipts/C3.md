# C3 | Competition at launch

## Decision and specification

C3 is an exploratory 36-month endpoint for K25. Count distinct applicants with a final original FDA ANDA approval in each at-risk active-moiety group's window. The event threshold is three applicants. Keep the criteria-v3 roster and the existing 24-month native M3 and B1 scores. The specified queue has `floor(N/5)` slots per cutoff and fractional expected credit for ties at the boundary. Unknown labels keep their original queue allocation. The four development cutoffs support a label audit. Only 1 January 2013 also belongs to the frozen 24-month validation schedule and has mature 36-month follow-up inside the guarded development period. ## Identity evidence and limits

Drugs@FDA carries a sponsor field at the application level. The openFDA record supplies the final original action date and application identifier. FDA action letters can identify the recipient of a specific regulatory action. A holder in a subsequent Orange Book edition or next-day snapshot may reflect a different ownership date. The outcome builder requires evidence covering the approval day. It uses an effective date interval for a documented transfer. It folds case, whitespace and canonical Unicode before applying dated, evidence-backed name aliases. The associated application count provides a finite upper bound even when its historical applicant is unavailable. [Drugs@FDA data files](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/drugsfda-data-files) · [FDA Orange Book field definitions](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/orange-book-data-files)

A qualifying outcome requires an original final ANDA. An authorized generic marketed under the original NDA falls outside the endpoint. The full active-moiety set can pool salts, formulations, routes, strengths, indications and reference products. Linked current-snapshot action histories can miss earlier changed or removed records. The bounds apply to recorded, linked qualifying approvals. Approved applicants measure regulatory crowding. Marketed producers, launches, geographic demand and narrower product segments require separate evidence. [FDA authorized generic listing](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/center-drug-evaluation-and-research-cder/fda-listing-authorized-generics) · [FIX2c](FIX2c.md)
