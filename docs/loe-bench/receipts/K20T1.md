Reuse DEVX's guarded linkage and the T4b pre-decode outcome projection with a final 2016-01-01 observation date. Require the seal lock and authorization variable to be absent. For K20, extend training to January 1 cutoffs from 1987 through the last cutoff that satisfies the split embargo. Prefer the freshest proven FDA ZIP within six calendar months. Otherwise use the latest NBER annual edition admitted by E8's publication or year-only rule, including the 1985 edition at the 1987 cutoff. Staleness is days since the ZIP edition date or a proxy for annual content through December 31 before the printed edition year. This latter approximation applies uniformly to the annual editions and can differ from documented title-page dates. Carry a separate NBER indicator. For absent editions use zero age plus a missing-edition indicator. Annual protection-only editions supply no strength or form/route breadth, so use the existing K07 missing breadth encoding. M1 and M3 receive the same regulatory inputs, with the existing verdict indicators added for M3. Keep L2, the five-fold validation cross-calibration, and the E7b action thresholds fixed during K20. B1 is refit in both configurations.

Compare AURC80 for each arm and all three contrasts (M3−B1, M3−M1, M1−B1). Use the exact tied-prefix rule and the same 2,000 paired whole-drug draws for all arms and contrasts within a fold and horizon. Draws use seed 20261127, percentile 95% intervals and report invalid replicates. Adopt K20 only if the primary candidate-minus-baseline change in M3−B1 is ≤ −0.0010 at 24 months and ≤ +0.0010 at 12 months, and the change in M1−B1 is ≤ +0.0010 at 24 months. An unevaluable gate fails. Run the DEVX forward split regardless of the primary decision and treat it as a descriptive robustness check. The forward validation rows overlap primary validation.

For T1, use the K20-selected training window. Define the inner slice as the latest primary TRAIN cutoff with a fresh FDA ZIP. Flag targets above the inner maximum or outside the three-point tolerance. Report achieved validation coverage, selective risk, per-class error among answered rows, and total and per-class referrals.
