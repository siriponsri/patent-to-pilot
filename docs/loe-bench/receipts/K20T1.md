## Failures and limitations

- The initial CLI smoke stopped before inputs because `devx_run.py` imports two T4 names that T4b renamed or removed. The card runner supplies compatibility patterns at import and uses the merged T4b projector. That smoke produced no metrics. The guarded retry reproduced the DEVX K07 baseline and completed both parts.
- K20 changes training era, sample count, source and missingness together. The trial estimates their combined effect. Annual staleness is a content-date proxy, and annual records lack presentation breadth. The early cutoffs can also depend on retrospective composition reconciliation. Raw NBER materials stay outside Git under the restrictions documented by E8.
- K20 intervals condition on fitted predictions. The forward check shares validation cutoffs with primary development. T1 uses a single fresh TRAIN cutoff for probability calibration and threshold selection and evaluates temporal transport into later years. The single-slice coverage success did not transfer reliably, and low selective risk coincided with all answered event rows being missed.
