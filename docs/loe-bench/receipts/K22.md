# K22 | Regulatory evidence graph trial

## Predeclared protocol

Use the retained K07/E7b primary development split from [K20T1](K20T1.md), from base `58f4f9ed45535bc6445868ab41b554a60392c290` with the merged REG challenge fix. Use the guarded DEVX identity and development-only outcome projection, observation through 2016-01-01, and an embargo requiring each training label window to close strictly before validation begins. Use the same rows, eligibility, raw features, challenge witnesses, K07 breadth, age base, logistic model and fixed L2 = 100 for all arms. Fit M1 once. Fit M3 twice with its ordinary verdict indicators supplied by v1 or v2 respectively. Evaluate the development validation rows for 24 and 12 months. Keep the test lock and authorization variable absent.

Use [criteria v3](../PLAN.md#confirmatory-criteria-v3-owner-approved-27-november-2026-supersedes-v2-endpoints). Rank each arm by its native entry-risk probability. At each annual cutoff allocate floor(N/5) review slots. Fractionally allocate a tie crossing the boundary and pool expected captured events and slots across cutoffs. Report recall, precision and pooled grouped-tie average precision. Report expected distinct event-drug capture at the observed cohort weights. For a drug with repeated positives, calculate one minus the product of its per-cutoff miss probabilities under independent boundary lotteries. For the secondary decision loss, reuse the [CP4](CP4.md) illustrative policy and costs: probability cutoffs lo = 0.05 and hi = 0.5, and M3 refers a high-risk BLOCKED conflict and clears other BLOCKED rows. For this secondary calculation, fit an arm-specific sigmoid to five whole-drug-fold out-of-fold TRAIN scores at the latest training cutoff, following the K20T1 calibration method. Charge 10 per missed clear, 1 per false flag and 0.5 per referral, with perfect referral adjudication assumed. Do not tune on validation.

For each horizon use 2,000 paired whole-drug bootstrap draws with NumPy seed 20261127. Sample the same drug multiplicities for all arms and recompute annual capacities in each draw. Report percentile 95% intervals and undefined draws for levels and for M3(v2) minus M3(v1), M3(v2) minus M1, and M3(v1) minus M1. Report OPEN, BLOCKED and CONTESTED counts per graph and horizon.

Adopt v2 only if M3(v2) minus M3(v1) recall is at least +0.02 at 24 months and at least −0.02 at 12 months, and v2 has fewer pooled CONTESTED validation rows across the two horizons. Otherwise retain v1. This rule uses point estimates. The intervals are descriptive development evidence.

The v2 version also reaches the M3 action policy. The same selected Orange Book edition and E7b challenge witnesses feed the raw features in both versions.

## Failures and limitations

- The first unittest discovery run found three errors in older callers and fixtures that supplied the original as-of fields. The fallback to v1 for these callers resolved all three. The rerun passed all 159 tests. Existing Purple Book temporary-file handle warnings were present in both runs.
- Repository-root `python -B -m pytest --tb=short --basetemp C:/Users/User/AppData/Local/Temp/ptp-k22-be41cb63 -p no:cacheprovider` exited 1 with 2,760 passed, 50 skipped, one xfailed and the eight accepted pre-existing failures. The failed nodes were the four budget variants each in `test_sacr_r2_smoke_supervisor.py` and `test_sacr_r3_smoke_supervisor.py`. [Repository test policy](../../../AGENTS.md)
- The actual statutory stay can start at notice receipt if suit meets the 45-day and relevant-patent requirements. The corpus lacks receipt dates, complete applicant and RLD links, case disposition and verified stay orders. The filing-date proxy is conditional. Its `FALSE` state means the challenge path is suppressed within that proxy window, and its `TRUE` state means that window has elapsed. Actual effective approval, shorter or longer stays, settlements and patent disposition remain unresolved. Patent expiry can still satisfy the separate patent path. Rai case availability at filing follows E7b's assumption. [Conditional 30-month stay](../REGULATORY-RULES.md#L26) · [E7b protocol](E7b.md#development-protocol)
- The earliest eligible product approval in the same NDA serves as a proxy for original approval. Product-specific scope, waiver by applicant, unknown GAIN/PED attachments, later-added patents and cross-presentation linkage remain sources of uncertainty. The trial concerns first final ANDA approval, as a proxy for entry. It has no external regulatory adjudication. [REG disposition](../REGULATORY-RULES.md#disposition-for-integration)
- The bootstrap resamples the fixed validation drug histories and predictions. It describes sampling variation within development given these fits. The decision loss uses illustrative costs and perfect referral adjudication. It does not establish the safety or workload acceptance of a deployed policy.
