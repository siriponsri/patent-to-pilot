## Failures and limitations

- The FDA list can omit or publish submissions late. An unresolved, undated or unmatched row supplies no witness. When the edition is fresh and verified, FALSE is an operational absence under this join. Dosage-form aggregation at the moiety level can conflate challenges to different formulations. TRUE only indicates a challenge filing. FDA approval and lawful entry depend on additional conditions.
- Rai's table has no ANDA case-type field. A retrospective Orange Book patent annotation alone is insufficient because patents can cover multiple products or turn up in unrelated litigation. The docket inventory is a bounded, manually corroborated subset. It excludes unverified actions, patent-only rows, PTAB proceedings and non-ANDA litigation from positive evidence. The source-row match rate therefore cannot estimate ANDA coverage. The original complaint bytes and public-access timestamps for the admitted cases were not independently archived in E7. Availability at filing follows the task assumption.
- District of Delaware's ANDA-specific Nature of Suit 835 code starts on 2017-06-01, after the development cutoffs. The USPTO 2016 public mirror has generic patent case types, so it did not supply an ANDA admission rule. The Justia PDF mirror for the XYREM order returned an access challenge in direct HTTP. The public FDA package supplied the docket corroboration instead. [Delaware notice](https://www.ded.uscourts.gov/sites/ded/files/news/NOS%20835%20ANDA.pdf) · [USPTO case code definitions](https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/Addendum-Litigation-Data-Final-2019-12-19.pdf)
- EDGAR was NOT_RUN. This card had no fixed sponsor universe or reviewed deterministic procedure for tying explicit Paragraph IV language to a product and its filing date across 10-K/10-Q layouts.
