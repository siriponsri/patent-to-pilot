## Prospective development protocol

Replay E7b on the same FIX2c cohort, embargo, horizons, tuning grid, calibration, actions and validation endpoints. The event is first final original ANDA approval within the horizon. Add the four PSG fields and explicit missingness flags to both M1 and M3. Keep B1 and M2 as controls. Use 2,000 paired drug-cluster draws for the three E7b arm comparisons. Report point differences from the pinned E7b summary for the same metrics and comparators. Keep cutoffs and label windows strictly before 2017-01-01.

Use the FDA alphabetical listing and official recent release tables. Audit the first successful CDX landing-page capture returned per year from 2010 through 2016. Include the earlier public CDX landing-page captures returned after collapsing repeated digests through the end of 2009, so the 2010-01-01 training rows can receive timely evidence. The landing pages cover recent changes and link to alphabetical inventories. Their omissions leave historical status unknown. Report coverage and continuity on observed rows only. Join historical to current listings by exact PDF identifier or normalized full composition, route, dosage form and overlapping RLD/RS application numbers. Prefer candidates supported by both paths when they agree. Record ambiguous links and report recommendation-date agreement at month resolution for uniquely linked rows, with a separate denominator for historically marked new rows.

Admit an archived PSG observation only if its capture and the end of its stated recommendation month are both on or before the cutoff. Reject captures dated before the stated recommendation month. Keep historical evidence independent of current-list survival. Count distinct observed PSGs by normalized full composition, route, form and RLD/RS application set, using the document ID when the application field is absent. An explicitly new posting supplies an observed first date. An explicit revision supplies a positive flag when its entire reported date interval falls in the trailing 12 calendar months. Treat unobserved status, first dates and revision status as unknown on partial coverage. Count seen guidance identities as a lower bound under stable listing identity. With a complete historical capture, its membership restricts the eligible identities. Keep current-list dates and audit linkage outside the historical feature builder.

E9 tests archived PSG availability at the LOE-Bench cutoff. For first ANDA submission in orphan drugs, Hopefl et al. used PSG status one year before the earliest lawful ANDA submission date for NCEs and within the first year after NDA approval for non-NCEs. Their random survival forest was compared with Cox and accelerated failure time models. [Hopefl et al., Section 3.1 and Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1111/cts.70365)

## Failures and limitations

- The current database reports version dates and a recent change batch. The archive sample covers recent updates only. Complete first-posting and revision histories, withdrawal dates and historical negatives remain unverified. The current inventory retains known dates and their locators. One 2015 archive row displayed `122004` and was excluded from date comparisons and the feature builder. A blank date in an earlier capture was also ineligible.
- E9 carries a positive archived listing forward until contrary evidence from a complete listing is available. Older sightings can therefore persist after withdrawal. Missed postings between captures, revised application numbers and name changes can lower coverage or split the same PSG across identities. Historical eligibility depends on archive capture and month-end bounds, so a listing may become usable after its actual publication date. The current listing cannot supply a historical positive by itself.
- FIX2c supplies a retrospective moiety crosswalk. Historical availability of those mappings is unverified. The model learns from age, evidence and PSG missingness on 2010 training rows. Performance can depend on which FDA releases the archive happened to preserve and which moieties had already entered the development cohort. Paired intervals condition on development predictions and tuning.
- The old CDER URL and the current database URL returned empty CDX results for 2010–2016. The older FDA landing URL supplied all seven annual captures and 20 earlier captures. An alphabetical-page CDX probe showed captures for A. A complete historical alphabetical edition was outside this replay.
- The first replay stopped before evaluation on a Git-for-Windows line-ending difference in the committed E7b JSON. The retry verifies the published JSON digest in its LF or CRLF encoding. The raw FDA, archive, Orange Book and court data still require exact byte digests.
- The second replay stopped at the 12-month control check because the metric emits tuple bin counts and the E7b JSON stores them as a list. The final replay compares both controls in their serialized representation.
