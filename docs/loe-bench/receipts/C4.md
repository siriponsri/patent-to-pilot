## Protocol freeze

[C4_SPEC.json](../../../loe-bench/data/C4_SPEC.json) and [C4_INPUTS.json](../../../loe-bench/data/C4_INPUTS.json) were committed before the forecast run. The spec pins the code commit, the manifest pins the exact SDUD and FDA bytes, and its outcome freeze declares separate quarter and file roles. The SHA-256 is computed from the exact LF Git blob for each tracked JSON lock. Raw source files use their original bytes.

The endpoint is the next four calendar quarters of covered outpatient prescription counts by FDA-listed ingredient, using published US Medicaid national `XX` data for 50 states and DC. A combination prescription contributes once to each listed ingredient. The model fits eight consecutive quarters through Q2 of the origin year, then projects across two quarters of assumed publication lag and totals steps 3 through 6. Both arms use the same ingredient-origin rows. Signed bias is the forecast minus the observed count. CMS's current schedule supports the Q2 proxy, without proving the historical release time for a particular file. Before 2010Q1, only fee-for-service utilization (FFSU) is reportable. Managed-care utilization (MCOU) enters from 2010Q1 and creates a reporting change in the early windows. [CMS national view](https://www.medicaid.gov/faq/2020-04-09/91786) · [CMS-R-144 state invoice definitions](https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid/prescription-drugs/downloads/cms-r-144-state-invoice-data-definitions-2019.pdf).

The study by Nikolopoulos et al. motivates the naïve-with-drift benchmark from UK prescription series. The publisher preview was accessible, and the full journal text required access. The quarterly history and zero floor are C4 design choices. The comparator is the last observed quarter carried forward. [Primary research paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2015.08.001) · [Drift formula](https://otexts.com/fpp3/toolbox.html#drift-method).

## Limits and planner handoff

- The openFDA snapshot is drawn from the current FDA NDC Directory. Delisted packages and historical label changes can be absent. The package date gate limits obvious misassignment. The historical crosswalk remains unverified. The reported link rates measure resolution to the LOE-Bench GSRS key format. Benchmark cohort membership remains unmeasured. [FDA NDC scope](https://open.fda.gov/data/ndc/) · [CMS NDC reuse guidance](https://www.medicaid.gov/prescription-drugs/downloads/mfr-rel-118.pdf) · [GSRS provenance](FIX2.md#sources).
- This estimates US Medicaid use where the evidence permits. Thai volume, other US payers, patients, sales and future market share are outside the endpoint. The Thai-context predecessor found public procurement, NLEM and NHSO sources with distinct units and incomplete ingredient-level coverage. [Thai open-data inventory](../THAI-OPEN-DATA.md).
- The main planner owns integration and the lane handoff and memory. Carry forward the vintage verdict and full-target failure rule. Before a registered C4 run, review dated byte-bound SDUD releases, historical package identities and whether complete counts can be reconstructed under CMS suppression. Keep the full national forecast unavailable when the gate fails. [D41 component plan](../PLAN.md#d41-market-window-dossier-owner-approved-28-november-2026).
