# Receipt E2: historical Orange Book editions (T6, gate G1)

## Result

**DONE.** 19 distinct Orange Book data-file editions (products, patent, exclusivity) cover edition dates from June 2009 to November 2026, in 16 calendar years. 18 came from the Wayback Machine and 1 was reused from earlier D37/B1E work. A leakage-safe as-of parser is in place. No model was scored on any outcome.

## Evidence

- **Reuse check.** The earlier "exact-edition pairs" in `docs/ra-pharma/research/ip-pharma/b1e/` are label and review editions, not Orange Book editions. The only existing Orange Book file is the 13 Nov 2026 ZIP, `_private_artifacts/ptp-ip-pharma-landscape-20261126/orange-book.zip` (SHA-256 `d4dbc9d8…7792241`, pinned in `docs/ra-pharma/ip-pharma/B1E-AMENDMENT.md`). It was copied unchanged and is listed in the manifest.
- **Sources.** Wayback captures of `fda.gov/downloads/Drugs/InformationOnDrugs/UCM163762.zip` (2009–2019), `fda.gov/media/76860/download` (2020–2026) and `…/download?attachment` (2024). Raw ZIPs, retrieved with Wayback's raw `id_` mode on 27 Nov 2026, are in `_private_artifacts/loe-bench/ob_editions/` and are not in Git.
- **Manifest.** [OB_EDITIONS.json](../../../loe-bench/data/OB_EDITIONS.json) records, for each edition, the source URL, Wayback timestamp, retrieval time, bytes, SHA-256, row counts per file and the search log. Every sha256 was re-verified at manifest build time.
- **Edition dates.** The edition date is the maximum modification date of the ZIP members, which is the FDA generation date. The Wayback capture is always later. The parser selects on the edition date only, so it is conservative about leakage.
- **Coverage by edition date.** 2009-06-09, 2009-12-22, 2011-03-18, 2012-01-17, 2013-01-18, 2014-09-10, 2015-01-16, 2016-03-18, 2016-12-16, 2018-01-12, 2019-01-18, 2020-11-02, 2021-02-12, 2022-02-11, 2023-01-13, 2024-02-09, 2025-01-10, 2026-07-10, 2026-11-13. Products grow from 24,135 to 48,990 rows.
- **Code.** [ob_history.py](../../../loe-bench/loe_bench/ob_history.py) provides `OBHistory.features_asof(appl_no_or_ingredient_key, cutoff)`. It uses only the latest edition with edition date ≤ cutoff and never loads a later one. It returns patent expiries, unexpired patent count, exclusivity codes and expiries, and the earliest patent Submission_Date. `dataset.py` is unchanged.
- **Tests.** `loe_bench/tests/test_ob_history.py` runs on small synthetic ZIP fixtures. They cover the future-edition leakage test (including the day-before and same-day boundaries), parsing of all three file types (headered and headerless), and missing edition giving `status=UNKNOWN`. Together with the existing 16 tests, 28 pass. The test over the real manifest ran, not skipped, on this machine.

## Failures and limitations

- **No challenge field.** Orange Book files have no paragraph-IV or challenge field. The only proxy, patent `Submission_Date`, exists from the 2018 editions on. Earlier editions report `submission_date_available=False` and `None`, never FALSE.
- **2009 format.** The 2009 editions are headerless and lack Appl_Type, so the parser keys on Appl_No and Product_No. The parser was not checked against any outcome.
- **Gaps.** No edition is dated 2010 or 2017. Wayback has further captures, including mid-year ones, that were not fetched, so the gaps in the dates above are not archive limits. The longest is 2019-01-18 to 2020-11-02, about 21 months. Nothing before June 2009 was obtained.
- **Failed attempts.** CDX found no captures for `accessdata.fda.gov/cder/ob/docs/obdata.zip`. Wayback also holds some non-ZIP captures, which I did not use: 403, 401, 301/302 and 404 for the `media/76860` URLs, plus one 655-byte body on 2024-03-11. All 18 downloads returned HTTP 200 on the first try.
- **Ingredient keys.** Ingredient-key lookup uses the crude `norm_ingredient` moiety key, as in `dataset.py`. Appl_No lookup is exact.
- **Descriptive only.** No numbers beyond coverage and row counts were computed.
