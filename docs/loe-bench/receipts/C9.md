## Limits to carry forward

The 2010 training cutoff had 25 positive drugs at 12 months and 46 at 24 months. The selected C9 training count, class balance and prospective event denominator remain unknown. Drug histories can recur across windows, and repeated batches add no independent outcomes. A single fitted seed and few prospective events limit interval interpretation. Historical outcomes may have appeared in checkpoint pretraining. A dated input projection and prospective lock limit information about the evaluated future outcomes. ForecastBench is a prospective-design precedent, and the prediction-market papers are evidence about other forecast settings. The direct LOE-Bench comparison is GRPO versus paired SFT. [K23 ledger](../TECHNIQUE-LEDGER.md) · [Turtel et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17989v4) · [Levy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15917v2) · [Paleka et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00723v1) · [ForecastBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19839v5)
