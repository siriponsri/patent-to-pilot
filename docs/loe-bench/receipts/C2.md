# C2 | L1e evidence-matched prompt

## Protocol recorded before inference

L1e is the exploratory C2 arm in [D41](../PLAN.md#d41-market-window-dossier-owner-approved-28-november-2026) and [K24](../TECHNIQUE-LEDGER.md). Use the pinned L1 model, tokenizer, parser, fp16, greedy decoding and batch size. Rebuild the validation roster with the guarded [FREEZE](FREEZE.md) readers and require the identity-only export to match the [L1DEV](L1DEV.md) byte hash. The 2010-01-01 TRAIN data are used locally to replay the B1 and M3 comparators. Validation uses 2012–2015 for 12 months and 2013–2014 for 24 months. Only validation identity and as-of evidence go to RunPod.

The prompt uses M3's source evidence, including the date clocks that feed graph v1 and the as-of challenge indicator. It excludes the graph verdict, verdict indicators, B1 hazard and logit, fitted risks, decisions and outcome fields. The model can infer its own forecast from the evidence. The comparison tests the effect of adding the frozen evidence presentation to L1 and is exploratory for the other comparators.

Require at least 180 schema-valid responses among the first 200 fixed forecasts. A failure stops generation. Require the entire verified roster after a gate pass for any paired result. For failed, incomplete or unverifiable runs mark the affected comparisons `NOT_RUN`. An invalid response abstains and ties at −1 across all invalid responses. Schema-valid abstentions retain `p_entry`. Reuse the L1 criterion of `floor(N/5)` per cutoff and fractional end-of-queue ties, queue precision and pooled grouped AP. Compare L1e with L1, M3 and B1 using 2,000 paired whole-drug draws per horizon with seed 20261127. Verify the three comparator levels and intervals against the published development results.

Prefer one L4 and the staged volume in US-MO-2. If that placement has no L4 stock, use one L4 in a live-quoted location and restage the same publisher- and digest-pinned files and dependency wheels on the pod disk. The stage and inference share one pod, with outputs pulled and verified before pod deletion. Use a wall-clock deadline plus cleanup reserve and cap the card at USD 4 including storage. Keep raw prompts and outputs outside Git. Test and preregistration release remain with the owner.

The synthetic fixtures compare every transmitted numeric value with `devx.model_inputs` for M3. They check a one-patent count with a later submission excluded, the latest patent and exclusivity dates, a known zero for elapsed exclusivity, and partial strength data with one observed presentation. A dated challenge produces `(present, missing) = (1, 0)`. An undated challenge produces `(0, 1)`, while verified list non-observation produces `(0, 0)`. Fixtures reject future witnesses and admit the frozen patent relinkage source. The response fixture checks the 179/200 and 180/200 boundary, a partial run after a gate pass, and native scores for valid abstentions and invalid responses. ## Limitations and handoff

- The exploratory result applies to the frozen prompt, Qwen checkpoint, development roster and review budget. The paired draws hold fitted comparators fixed. The change from L1 includes an evidence-only instruction as well as the added evidence fields. Pretraining overlap with retrospective approval records remains unknown.
- The host changed from US-MO-2 to EU-RO-1. The run used the same GPU class, image, model, tokenizer, wheel pins and decoding settings. Cross-host output reproducibility remains unmeasured. Transformers warned about unused temperature, top-p and top-k under greedy decoding.
- The main planner is the sole reviewer. Review the negative development result and decide how C2 may be described in preregistration v2. The test seal stayed closed. Owner authorization governs preregistration and test release.
