# JURIS-COUNT feasibility receipt

Canada yielded **33 observed first qualifying original ANDS notices of compliance (NOCs)** over calendar 2023–2025, including **27** whose active-moiety sets had no matching eligible US event in the pooled interval. Japan yielded **11 dated listing witnesses linked to dated new-active-ingredient approvals**, plus a reproducible national NHI code-family screen. Japanese firstness at the full active-moiety-set grain remains unresolved. Neither country has demonstrated a complete proof of what was public at each historical cutoff. These are descriptive feasibility results for planner review under the [decision rule](../JURISDICTION-DECISION-RULE.md), [feasibility card](../JURISDICTION-FEASIBILITY.md) and [power plan](../POWER-PLAN.md).

## Japan · day-level evidence and coverage limits

The PMDA 2022–2025 fiscal-year workbooks span all three calendar years. The parser retains new-active-ingredient original approval rows, including Excel serial and Reiwa dates. It finds 30/64/44 labelled rows in 2023/2024/2025, including 11/23/13 with an explicit biological marker in the ingredient field. The remaining 19/41/31 are a modality review pool and still include possible biological products, multiple presentations or components that need adjudication.

The MHLW claims-master screen found 65/28/28 observed YJ seven-character code families whose earliest **dated** listed generic row falls in the three years. Within those, 47/17/15 families have another generic code with no verified listing day. They cannot establish code-family firstness. The remaining **18/11/13** provide dated code-family candidates with no unresolved earlier generic date for any code observed within the four editions. These are sampling frames at a billing-code grain. Ingredient equivalence across formulations, products no longer in any of the four editions, products outside the add-on flag, separate brand code assignment and generic authorization or licensing status all affect eligibility. January–February 2023 predates field 36 coverage. An old zero in field 36 cannot be assigned a listing date from a later pricing change. The worksheet and master records therefore leave the national at-risk denominator and annual eligible event counts **NOT_ESTIMABLE**.

The [witness ledger](../../../loe-bench/data/JURIS-COUNT/japan-witnesses.csv) documents a targeted 17-row review. The December 2024 council table supplied 13 strength-level rows. The ledger distinguishes the two sugammadex listings for one moiety set and excludes the ustekinumab biosimilar. Other unresolved rows include a combination whose original class needs confirmation, ropivacaine whose emergency listing day is established but original NAS class needs confirmation, and first-by-route or strength records for metronidazole and epinastine. In December 2025, lacosamide and abiraterone generic additions took effect on 5 December. The related originator reimbursement annex took effect on 1 January 2026. Keep the dates separate.
