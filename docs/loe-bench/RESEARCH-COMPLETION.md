## What counts as 100%

1. **Method evidence.** Every registered hypothesis, secondary endpoint, exploratory comparison and pilot has a receipt with its cohort, denominator, estimate and uncertainty where defined, or its registered stopping, missingness or non-execution reason. A failed test, undefined denominator or justified NOT_RUN closes that analysis. A pending executable deliverable stays open. A later exploratory supplement cannot change the fixed sequence. 2. **Reproducibility and archive.** Someone outside the owner’s environment can retrieve exact shareable forecast bytes from a version DOI, verify them against the timestamped manifest, and exercise the scorer from a clean environment. Restricted components have rights, recovery instructions and an honest availability status. The guide assigns the two future readouts or records that nobody has accepted custody. This closes the present obligation while the actual prospective outcomes remain pending. [Endgame](ENDGAME-PLAN.md) · [Scoring guide](HOW-TO-SCORE-TRACK-A.md)

A blocked submission can close the January reporting obligation only when recorded as such. Acceptance is outside the completion criterion. [Schedule](PLAN.md) · [Endgame](ENDGAME-PLAN.md)
