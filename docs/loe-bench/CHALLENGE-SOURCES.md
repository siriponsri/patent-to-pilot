# Public evidence of a generic patent challenge at an annual cutoff

**Recommendation for LOE-Bench.** For gaps in E6's historical FDA list, start with sponsor disclosures filed on EDGAR. Use the open Rai et al. district-court table to locate candidate cases, then admit only facts supported by a public document available by the cutoff. The first route yielded a pre-approval witness for Eliquis. The second supplied product-to-docket links but needs an independently dated witness for early cutoffs. Neither sample measures cohort coverage or forecast improvement. All external sources below were retrieved on **27 November 2026**. The [receipt](receipts/E6alt.md) identifies the editions, hashes, failed access attempts and checks.

## One drug across three annual cutoffs

| Cutoff | What the checked documents support for Eliquis tablets |
|---|---|
| 2016-12-31 | The December 2015 FDA patent-extension notice identifies apixaban and NDA 202155. The notice contains no generic challenge. Leave the leaf UNKNOWN on this evidence. [Federal Register, p. 76697](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2015-12-10/pdf/2015-31096.pdf) |
| 2017-12-31 | Pfizer's August 10 filing describes 25 generic firms sending Paragraph IV notices and April lawsuits. This supports an observed challenge for the identified brand by August 10. Preserve the reported February–April interval separately. The filing does not assign a challenge to every strength or give each ANDA number. [10-Q, PDF p. 36](https://s206.q4cdn.com/795948973/files/doc_financials/2017/q2/10-Q.pdf) · [EDGAR accession and acceptance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/78003/000007800317000039/0000078003-17-000039-index.htm) |
| 2019-12-31 | FDA lists two first final approvals on December 23. This is an outcome for the stated endpoint. The 2019 approvals cannot supply features to either earlier cutoff. Sigmapharm's separate tentative letter in 2020 concerns another ANDA for the same product. [FDA, rows 103–104](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/first-generic-drug-approvals/2019-first-generic-drug-approvals) · [Sigmapharm, pp. 1–2](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2020/210053Orig1s000TAltr.pdf) |

Record the asserted event, its date or interval, the public witness date, the retrieval date and the product scope separately. Require both a product match and a dated public witness for the exact assertion. For a date given only to month precision, use month-end for conservative admission. If the public clock is unknown or falls after the cutoff, leave the leaf UNKNOWN. A certification, a filed lawsuit and an FDA determination of exclusivity are distinct events. Track the related filings under one product challenge when counting presence. Later settlements, approvals and marketing dates cannot enter an earlier prediction. These are proposed admission rules for review.

## Source assessment

GREEN means the checked record supports a dated positive. AMBER means that the source needs an additional date, text or product check. RED means the indicated use would mistake another event for a challenge or reveal the forecast outcome.

### 1. EDGAR sponsor filings · GREEN for explicit disclosures

**Coverage and access.** US reporting issuers, with issuer-dependent product coverage. EDGAR indexes public filings from 1994 Q3. Its nightly submissions metadata ZIP and per-CIK JSON histories locate accession, form and primary document. Daily archive files include filing text. Inspect the litigation note itself and use the public dissemination day. A late acceptance can move dissemination to the next business day. SEC does not publish the instant a document first appeared on its website. At a cutoff boundary use the dated public index or a contemporaneous copy. Reporting-period end, private notice and lawsuit may all precede filing. SEC says public filing content is free to access and reuse. Follow its published fair-access terms. [EDGAR coverage, daily files and terms of access](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data) · [SEC API](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) · [SEC reuse FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/files/about/webmaster-faq.htm)

**Two records checked.** Pfizer 10-Q 0000078003-17-000039 was accepted August 10, 2017 at 16:09:11. Pfizer 10-K 0000078003-18-000027 was accepted February 22, 2018 at 15:42:15. Both identify the US Eliquis notices and litigation. Only the first filing qualifies at year-end 2017. Match jurisdiction and brand to the dated FDA NDA record before rolling up to ingredient. Treat the repeated report as one positive. Selective issuer reporting makes silence uninformative. [Quarterly report, PDF p. 36](https://s206.q4cdn.com/795948973/files/doc_financials/2017/q2/10-Q.pdf) · [quarterly index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/78003/000007800317000039/0000078003-17-000039-index.htm) · [annual report, PDF p. 268](https://s206.q4cdn.com/795948973/files/doc_financials/2017/q4/Form-10-K-2017.pdf) · [annual index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/78003/0000078003-18-000027-index.htm) · [FDA product identity](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2015-12-10/pdf/2015-31096.pdf)

### 2. Academic court-case data · AMBER for case discovery

Rai, Vishnubhakat, Lemus and Hovenkamp released version 1.0 on June 10, 2021 under CC0. The public Dataverse API exposes individual tab-separated files. The observed filing-date range in `LM_short_patent_cases.tab` is January 1, 2011 through June 30, 2020. Fields include civil action, court, filing date, Orange Book brand and ingredients, patent, case status, termination and resolution. Fetch file 4789869 without a Lex Machina account. The authors describe how they assembled the court and PTAB data. Preserve each forum separately. [Data DOI](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/YCMKVU) · [version, licence and files](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/datasets/:persistentId/?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/YCMKVU) · [court-case table](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/4789869) · [Rai et al., methods and note 79](https://lawcat.berkeley.edu/record/1257859/files/0003-37-1-Rai.pdf)

**Three case–patent rows checked.** D. Del. 1:17-cv-00374, April 5, 2017, appears twice for ELIQUIS/apixaban, patents 6967208 and 9326945. D. Del. 1:17-cv-00412, April 10, 2017, also lists apixaban and 6967208. The two patents in the first docket establish one candidate action. The 2021 table cannot establish public knowledge in April 2017. Resolve docket, patent, product and public date against a contemporaneous complaint, filing or other dated source. Omit termination, result and present status from the historical feature. [Named rows, file 4789869](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/4789869) · [Delaware opinion, consolidated case](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ded-1_17-cv-00374/pdf/USCOURTS-ded-1_17-cv-00374-3.pdf)

### 4. Published opinions and open docket copies · AMBER

**GovInfo.** Selected federal courts, generally from 2004 with incomplete early holdings. Enumerate the USCOURTS sitemap and fetch opinion PDF and MODS metadata by package and granule. Read the matched opinion's issue date and the repository ingestion date separately. Ingestion describes preservation and can be package-level. Require a dated public issue, public docket entry, or capture for the relevant text. Sealed complaints and later unsealings require the public version. Government works generally permit reuse subject to third-party material. [Collection and URL scheme](https://www.govinfo.gov/help/uscourts) · [sitemaps](https://www.govinfo.gov/sitemaps) · [GPO terms](https://www.govinfo.gov/about/policies)

Two checks illustrate the lag. D. Del. 1:17-cv-00374, opinion -3, issued August 5, 2020, records apixaban litigation including Sigmapharm ANDA 210053. Package ingestion was March 26, 2021. N.D. Ill. 1:14-cv-10150, opinion -0, issued February 10, 2016, recounts the Impax Opana ER certification as an allegation. Its returned package XML shows ingestion July 28, 2022 and later opinions. Neither package date proves when the original complaint became public. Capture the paragraph and posture along with the matched product. [Eliquis opinion](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ded-1_17-cv-00374/pdf/USCOURTS-ded-1_17-cv-00374-3.pdf) · [Eliquis MODS](https://www.govinfo.gov/metadata/granule/USCOURTS-ded-1_17-cv-00374/USCOURTS-ded-1_17-cv-00374-3/mods.xml) · [Opana opinion, p. 7](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ilnd-1_14-cv-10150/pdf/USCOURTS-ilnd-1_14-cv-10150-0.pdf) · [Opana MODS](https://www.govinfo.gov/metadata/granule/USCOURTS-ilnd-1_14-cv-10150/USCOURTS-ilnd-1_14-cv-10150-0/mods.xml)

**CourtListener / RECAP.** The Free Law Project documents quarterly bulk snapshots and describes its bulk data as free of known copyright restrictions. The anonymous v4 search request returned 401. Two openly readable reporter entries tested identity matching. Pfizer v. Supp. 3d 1354, filed February 7, 2017, concerns product liability. Reject that brand-name hit. The sampled reporter records span 2006–2017. Complete annual RECAP coverage and two complaint-level public posting dates were not established. Join on court and docket, then confirm in the document. ### 5. Federal Register · GREEN for explicit product-specific notices

GovInfo provides issues and bulk XML via the FR sitemaps. Use publication day, issuing agency, docket, printed page and product name. The 1994-onward electronic collection includes many document types. These notices supply isolated observations and identity crosswalks. Apply GPO's government-work and third-party terms. [Federal Register collection](https://www.govinfo.gov/help/fr) · [GovInfo sitemaps](https://www.govinfo.gov/sitemaps) · [terms](https://www.govinfo.gov/about/policies)

Two examples have different meanings. The FTC's March 13, 2003 notice describes Paragraph IV filings and related Taxol lawsuits as allegations in an enforcement matter (68 FR 12083). This establishes a publicly reported challenge by that date. FDA's December 10, 2015 notice (80 FR 76697, FDA-2014-E-0279) links Eliquis to apixaban and NDA 202155, but concerns patent **extension**. It supplies the product match only. Preserve the agency's assertion and its publication date. [FTC notice, printed p. 12083](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2003-03-13/pdf/FR-2003-03-13.pdf) · [FDA notice, printed p. 76697](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2015-12-10/pdf/2015-31096.pdf)

## When FDA made Paragraph IV information public

| Era | Fields, cadence and consequence |
|---|---|
| **Pilot, by mid-2000** | Hemphill and Sampat reconstructed the monthly series from mid-2000. FDA's February 27, 2004 reply to petitions 99P-2778 and 00P-1556 independently describes a monthly pilot with generic name, RLD trade name, dosage form and **RLD** strengths. The 1999 petition was a request. The exact first publication day is unverified. [Authors' reconstruction, PDF p. 22](https://www.law.nyu.edu/sites/default/files/ECM_PRO_064165.pdf) · [FDA signed response, pp. 1–2, petitioner's copy](https://www.thefdalawblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/00p-1556-pav00001-vol11.pdf) |
| **From March 2, 2004** | FDA added the first substantially complete submission date prospectively and described reporting strengths challenged. Its 2004 response explains the delay while FDA determines whether to receive an application. FDA's 2017 draft Q&A describes updates twice monthly. FDA's historical field summary also identifies the RLD/NDA. A pre-MMA entry can omit a usable event date. [FDA response, pp. 4–6](https://www.thefdalawblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/00p-1556-pav00001-vol11.pdf) · [2017 draft, PDF p. 30](https://www.fda.gov/media/102650/download) · [FDA list documentation](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/abbreviated-new-drug-application-anda/patent-certifications-and-suitability-petitions) |
| **From June 18, 2019** | FDA added the count of potential first-applicant ANDAs, 180-day decision status and posting date, first-applicant approval date, first commercial marketing, and last qualifying patent expiration. Earlier rows can be backfilled. Later blanks generally indicate a pending decision or event. FDA describes regular updates with discretion to delay an entry. A guaranteed present-day interval was unverified. Keep the edition date and, for decisions, their posting month. [FDA column definitions](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/abbreviated-new-drug-application-anda/patent-certifications-and-suitability-petitions) |
