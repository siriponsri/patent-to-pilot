# Prospective power for LOE-Bench

**Owner decision draft, 28 November 2026.** The existing Track A roster can detect a large gain over age alone. Its chance of detecting a small incremental graph benefit is low even before the H1 gate. I recommend a separate US rolling-origin study with one primary question: does M3 capture more future approvals than M1 at the same review capacity? The immediate investment is a local CPU feasibility simulation. A decade of prospective collection is a substantive custody commitment. Approval of feasibility does not authorize an OSF filing. [Owner decision, final entry](../maintenance/OWNER-DECISIONS-20261122.md) · [Endgame](ENDGAME-PLAN.md)

## What one frozen roster can resolve

Track A fixed 651 eligible active-moiety histories and 130 slots per arm at the 27 November 2026 cutoff. At 24 months, H1 tests M3 minus B1 and H2 tests M3 minus M1. H2 requires H1 success. H3 uses the frozen 12-month forecasts and requires both successes. Each reached test requires a strictly positive one-sided lower bound at 0.025, using 2,000 common whole-drug bootstrap draws, seed 20261127, linear quantiles and recomputed queues with fractional boundary ties. An undefined draw makes its interval unevaluable. The 2027 readout is descriptive and the sequence runs in November 2028. The registered +10 percentage-point (pp) practical threshold applies alongside every test. [Protocol](PREREGISTRATION.md) · [Scorer, panel and sequence](../../loe-bench/loe_bench/tracka_statistics.py) · [Scoring guide](HOW-TO-SCORE-TRACK-A.md)

The event count is unknown. The development validation rate, 91/1,083, gives 55 events when scaled to 651. The mature 24-month training windows give 651 × 618/7,893 = 51, and the latest window gives 651 × 46/615 = 49. These are overlapping historical windows with a different age and calendar mix. Use 51 for illustration and 32–70 for sensitivity. Each event on the single roster belongs to one drug. Extra slots or repeated rows do not create extra first approvals. [T1v3, development results](receipts/T1v3.md) · [TRACKA-CORE, training table](receipts/TRACKA-CORE.md)

**Planning calculation.** Let E denote independent-event equivalents and V the effective variance per event of the paired recall difference, including instability of the queue boundary. Approximate marginal power by Φ(δ√(E/V) − 1.960), with δ in recall units. The assumptions are stable event mix, constant V over the stated local effects and an approximately normal paired statistic. For H1 use V = 0.20: DEV gives 67 × 0.055497² = 0.206, and the +10 pp T4c reference gives about 50% power at 75 events. For H2, the same DEV calculation gives 0.027. Sparse disagreement, variable ranks at the last slot and temporal shifts can invalidate that transfer. Use a working stress value of 0.10, with 0.03 and 0.06 as sensitivities. None is an estimated variance for the future cohort. [T4c, variance and power](receipts/T4c.md) · [Field and Welsh, clustered resampling](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9868.2007.00593.x)

| 24-month effect scenario | 32 events | 51 events | 70 events |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| H1 +10 pp, registered practical threshold | 24% | 36% | 46% |
| H1 +20.37 pp, opened C′ point estimate | 73% | 90% | 97% |
| H1 +24.08 pp, T1v3 DEV | 86% | 97% | >99% |
| H2 −1.10 pp, T1v3 DEV | 1.6% | 1.4% | 1.2% |
| H2 +1.36 pp, opened C′ point estimate | 4.3% | 4.9% | 5.5% |
| H2 +2 pp, T4c design alternative | 5.5% | 6.6% | 7.6% |
| H2 +5 pp, proposed expansion design alternative | 14% | 20% | 26% |

H2 entries are marginal probabilities of declaring positive superiority. The probability of passing the sequence cannot exceed either marginal power. For H1 +20.37 pp and H2 +1.36 pp at 51 events, the marginals alone bound joint success between 0 and 4.9%. At H2 +5 pp and 51 events, changing V from 0.10 to 0.03 raises marginal power from 20% to about 54%. These are rounded design scenarios. Small numbers of discordant event drugs can make the normal approximation poor. C′ point estimates appear only to answer the sensitivity question. They supply no tuning, variance estimates, additional observations or new confirmatory evidence. [Historical score](receipts/SCORE.md) · [Independent aggregate replay](receipts/REPRO-C1.md) · [Joint interpretation](JOINT-SIGNOFF.md)

**Simulation cross-check.** T4c actually rebuilt queues on synthetic multi-cutoff histories. At +10/+2 pp for H1/H2, 50 event drugs yielded 36.76% H1 power, 5.55% H2 power conditional on reaching it and 2.04% joint power. At 75 the corresponding rates were 49.96%, 7.89% and 3.94%. At 150 they were 80.58%, 16.88% and 13.60%. At 75 events, increasing H2 to +5 pp yielded 27.26% joint power. The simulation's H1 null randomizes within B1 ties and the simulated quantile convention differs slightly from Track A. A one-origin Track A power simulation has yet to run. T4 and T4b addressed the superseded endpoint. [T4c, power and limitations](receipts/T4c.md) · [T4b](receipts/T4b.md) · [T4](receipts/T4.md)

## Information and calendar time

Use +5 pp as the proposed minimum worthwhile graph gain for sizing: at fixed workload it captures one additional first approval per 20 event drugs, roughly 2.5 per 51. This is a proposed operating judgment, independent of the opened result. It neither changes the registered +10 pp benchmark nor establishes economic benefit. Test superiority against zero and report a separate interval against +5 pp. A claim of a gain of at least five points would require its lower bound to clear five.

| Contrast and working V | Independent-event equivalents for 80% | For 90% |
|---|---:|---:|
| H1 +10 pp, 0.20 | 157 | 211 |
| H2 +5 pp, 0.03 | 95 | 127 |
| H2 +5 pp, 0.06 | 189 | 253 |
| H2 +5 pp, 0.10 | 314 | 421 |
| H2 +2 pp, 0.10 | 1,963 | 2,627 |

Counts round up V × (1.960 + z_power)² / δ² using unrounded quantiles. At 51 genuinely independent events per 24-month origin, 314/421 need at least 7/9 nonoverlapping origins (14/18 years of observation). Quarterly windows share 21 of 24 months. With stationary incidence and complete coverage, the distinct-event opportunity grows roughly as 51 + (K−1) × 51/8 over K quarterly origins: about 43/60 origins for 314/421 events, or 12.5/16.75 years through maturity. An event drug must also be eligible at a forecast origin. Unequal repeat weights, new origin eligibility, queue churn and common regulatory shocks change effective information. Calendar arithmetic is a feasibility screen, never a stopping rule or a guarantee. For H1 alone, 4/5 nonoverlapping origins are the analogous 80/90% benchmarks. For the original gated H2, marginal 80/90% is necessary and may be insufficient.

Fixed-queue paired comparisons depend heavily on how many event drugs the arms place on opposite sides of the review boundary. Before simulating the original roster, measure the M1/M3 overlap from frozen predictions alone, report the maximum possible capture difference at each assumed event count, and use synthetic event assignments compatible with those queues. Separately simulate boundary movement under whole-roster resampling. This provides a check on the wide variance range above without consulting prospective labels. [Connor, matched-pair power](https://doi.org/10.2307/2531961)

| 1 | Extend US calendar coverage with prospectively sealed origins | Highest direct gain from new first approvals. Quarterly freezes cost four CPU runs/year. Annual or biennial origins reduce maintenance, monthly origins mainly repeat drugs. Compare cadences over the same calendar span. The US COVID-19 Forecast Hub demonstrates timestamped rolling submissions and a common baseline. Its performance is external precedent only. | 2 | Pair arms and preserve histories | Immediate, low-cost precision gain over treating arms or origins as independent. Track A already pairs. | 6 | Sequential looks | Can save elapsed time for a strong effect. Repeated looks create no new events. Overlapping outcomes complicate information fractions. ## Proposed registration and feasibility gate

1. **Study and clock.** Name the extension “US rolling-origin graph comparison.” Start 1 April 2027, then 1 July, 1 October, 1 January and repeat through 1 October 2037 (43 origins). Final maturity is 1 October 2039. This candidate reaches approximately 319 distinct-event opportunities at central incidence. It targets 80% at +5 pp subject to simulation. The 90% stress-case target would require about 60 quarterly origins and a longer custody agreement. Infer for the fixed calendar schedule. Overlap in calendar time and drugs with the original Track A precludes calling the two independent replications. Exclude the November 2026 origin from every expansion statistic.
2. **Forecast issuance.** Reuse frozen fitted B1/M1/M3 core weights, identity rules, graph v1, native scores, cohort rules and the six-month, availability-proven Orange Book rule. No refitting. Capture public sources in advance. Specify the feature/eligibility snapshot as of the preceding UTC day using the latest verified extract actually available when sealing. Record effective cutoff, source lag and digest. Timestamp the roster, predictions and release manifest publicly before 00:00 UTC on the origin date. Use the frozen pre-origin roster without retrospective removals. Apply the declared first-final ORIG/AP identity and baseline de-duplication rules, audit late-reported pre-origin approvals separately, and restrict events to dates strictly after the origin through its 24-month anniversary. The same rules apply to every arm. [Track A core](receipts/TRACKA-CORE.md) · [Frozen release](receipts/TRACKA-FREEZE.md) · [Update 4](OSF-UPDATE-4-SHEET.md)
3. **Estimand and test.** For drug i and origin j, let Y be its event indicator and w_a its arm-specific fractional inclusion in the top floor(N_j/5). Primary Δ = ΣY(w_M3−w_M1)/ΣY over all forecast rows. This event-row-weighted pooled contrast gives repeated predictions of the same approval their intended forecasting weight. Report distinct approvals, repeats per drug, per-origin denominators and estimates. Resample unique drugs with replacement across all their origins and both arms, recomputing each origin's capacity and ties in every draw. Use PCG64 seed 20270401, 2,000 draws and linear quantiles. Reject Δ ≤ 0 only if the 2.5th percentile is strictly positive with all draws defined. Inference conditions on the named dates. Calendar shocks shared across drugs limit claims beyond these dates. Report leave-one-calendar-year-out sensitivity without hypothesis tests. [Field and Welsh](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9868.2007.00593.x)
4. **Multiplicity and stopping.** One extension primary hypothesis, final one-sided α = 0.025. Spend zero at every other date. B1 contrasts, individual origins, intervals against +5/+10 pp and unique-drug summaries are descriptive. Keep the original OSF family separate. Report both studies regardless of direction and make no program-wide “either study wins” claim. No interim efficacy, futility, observed-power looks, endpoint substitution, sample-size adjustment or outcome-dependent continuation. Operational staff may refresh coverage and report missing files. After the final origin matures, use the first complete verified outcome release covering maturity, preserve revisions separately, and report lateness. An unavailable maturity archive remains pending.
5. **Simulation before filing.** Lock simulation inputs, generator, candidate comparisons, seeds and gates before execution. Use guarded pre-2017 DEV and wholly synthetic prospective histories only. Reproduce the T4c 50/75/150-event reference under its own settings first (within 3 pp of published H1 and H2 sequence rates). Separately simulate the exact original single-origin sequence, including H3, to report marginal, reached and joint power. For the expansion compare quarterly, monthly, annual and biennial origins over the same start/end calendar span, always including the final origin. Also report shorter quarterly spans of 4 and 8 years. Treat these as feasibility comparisons for the owner, with quarterly/43 as the sole prespecified filing candidate in this proposal.
6. **Synthetic operating characteristics.** Generate new arrivals, absorbing first approvals, shared drug effects, common calendar shocks and correlated native arm ranks with B1 ties. Fit nuisance parameters on DEV only. Stress incidence at 32/51/70 per two years, within-drug correlations 0.5/0.8/0.95, calendar-year log-hazard shock SD 0/0.30 and graph effects 0/+2/+5/+10 pp. Use exact ties and recompute queues. Include broad cross-rank equal-capture nulls as well as within-tie nulls. Calibrate each scenario on independent synthetic histories to within 0.005 of its specified recall difference (null by exchangeability or analytic construction). Use independent PCG64 streams from seeds 2026112801 for calibration and 2026112802 for evaluation. Count unevaluable runs as failures to reject. Record rejection counts, Monte Carlo intervals, realized discordance, unique events, effective variance, evaluability, CPU time and failed calibrations.
7. **Proceed or stop.** Screen designs with 2,000 simulated panels per cell. Verify the filing candidate on independent seed 2026112803, 10,000 panels per designated verification cell, 2,000 bootstrap draws each. Verify all null stress cells, and +5 pp at central incidence, correlation 0.8 and both shock settings. Require a Wilson 95% upper limit ≤0.030 in every null cell (simulation tolerance around nominal 0.025), lower limit ≥0.80 for power and ≥0.99 for evaluability in both central alternatives. Report low-incidence power even if unfavorable. A failed gate, infeasible CPU budget, materially superior alternative cadence, or lack of a viable custodian returns the proposal to the owner before registration. Options include a newly locked design or descriptive surveillance. The thresholds are design checks, not proof of uniform error control.

**Operational power.** Under the full-panel rule below, even 99% independent on-time reliability per origin gives only 0.99⁴³ ≈ 65% probability of completing the panel. An 80%-powered analysis conditional on complete capture then has about a 52% chance of delivering a positive result at its design effect. Estimate this separately from statistical power, stress correlated outages, and compare with annual freezing. Bring both probabilities to the owner. If yearly origins give comparable power and higher completion probability, return that lower-maintenance design for review before filing.
