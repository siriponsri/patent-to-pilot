# C9 · Prospective GRPO versus supervised fine-tuning

### Copy for Secondary and exploratory analyses

On prospective Track A, compare one group relative policy optimization (GRPO) adapter with one supervised fine-tuning (SFT) adapter initialized from identical Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct weights and tokenizer (Apache-2.0, revision `989aa7980e4cf806f80c7fef2b1adb7bc71aa306`). The 1.54-billion-parameter checkpoint takes approximately 3.1 GB for its bfloat16 base weights. Freezing those weights, fitting small adapters and bounding the rollout count are the basis for planning a single 24 GiB GPU. Verify fit and throughput in January before proceeding. The paired comparison belongs to the exploratory analyses, outside H1–H3. Use the 24-month outcome of first dated final original FDA ANDA approval for the active-moiety set in `(cutoff, cutoff + 24 months]`. SFT minimizes completion-only token cross-entropy against the deterministic JSON assistant answer with `p_entry` equal to the binary label, a matching entry or no-entry action and an empty `missing_evidence` list. GRPO samples four continuations per prompt at temperature 1 with up to 160 tokens. For a valid output with probability `p` and resolved label `y`, reward is `−(p−y)²`. A malformed output receives −1.25. Use within-prompt standardized advantages, a 0.2 ratio clip and a 0.04 KL penalty to the frozen base. Use each finite adapter immediately after update 64. Generate one greedy JSON completion per drug with up to 160 new tokens, stopping at assistant EOS or the cap. Apply the pinned parser. **Interpretation.** The checkpoint may have encountered historical drug outcomes in pretraining, and training and prospective evaluation may include the same drug at different dates. Prospective timestamps and the source fence address temporal leakage into the future outcome comparison. The 2010-cutoff inventory recorded 25 positive drugs at 12 months and 46 at 24 months, and the C9 roster count is pending. The two objectives also differ in the supervision they can extract from a single binary outcome and may produce concentrated probabilities. Turtel et al. trained on roughly 110,000 market and generated questions and used Brier score. Levy examined Qwen2.5-1.5B GRPO in event forecasting, with market-probability cross-entropy in the main comparison. Those tasks supply external precedents. The same-checkpoint SFT control is the direct comparator for this LOE-Bench arm.
