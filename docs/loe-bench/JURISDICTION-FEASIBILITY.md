# Which jurisdiction should follow the United States?

**Co-planner decision memo · 28 November 2026. Keep Canada as the first feasibility card.** Its regulator distinguishes new active substances, original abbreviated submissions and supplements, and supplies ingredient-linked patent expiries. Japan is the strongest Asian alternative for a separate first **reimbursement listing** study. Singapore is the best ASEAN candidate for an **approval** study if the application-class history becomes openly reusable. No jurisdiction has passed a historical replay or yielded a measured increase in power here.

The US benchmark starts with a small-molecule NCE NDA and follows the active-moiety set to its first final original ANDA. Each origin has a 20% review queue. A second regulator creates a new local outcome. The same molecule can experience both outcomes, so uncertainty must account for shared drugs and repeated forecast origins. The existing planning estimate of 314–421 independent-event equivalents for a +5 percentage-point gain illustrates why even a large product count can add little power. ## National sources and admission decisions

**Canada.** The English/French weekly NOC extract and documented JSON/XML API cover human decisions since 1994. Join the notice, product and medicinal-ingredient tables. Start with original NDS/NAS (including priority NAS), then find the earliest original human small-molecule ANDS with the same moiety set. Inspect administrative, supplement and conditional flags, and decide how to handle exceptions before counting. DIN identifies a presentation. Map the ingredient names separately. Health Canada's [API example](https://health-products.canada.ca/api/documentation/noc-documentation-en.html?wbdisable=true) for NOC 403 demonstrates a supplement that should drop out. [Schema](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/drug-products/notice-compliance/database/read-file.html) · [NAS terminology](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/drug-products/notice-compliance/database/terminology.html)

The Patent Register offers a CSV download with medicinal ingredient, patent and expiry. The 13 November 2026 Register of Innovative Drugs gives NOC, six-year no-file and protection-end dates, including expired entries. Legacy 1991–93 NOC files exist. For example, the 1993 prescription list includes packaging changes. A weekly refreshed extract and a dated notice alone cannot establish which fields, corrections or patent listings were visible at an earlier origin. OGL Canada v2 permits “Copy, modify, publish, translate, adapt, distribute” with attribution. NOC and patent catalogue records identify their licence. Check the separate innovative-drug register's reuse grant and archive coverage before admitting that feature. [NOC catalogue](https://open.canada.ca/data/info/67995d41-0928-4a9f-b012-f38a90036812) · [Patent data](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/1674baf5-6822-4788-afb3-f8659531d7c0) · [Innovative drugs](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/drug-products/applications-submissions/register-innovative-drugs.html) · [Legacy listings](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/drug-products/notice-compliance/listings.html) · [OGL](https://open.canada.ca/open-government-licence-canada)

**Japan.** PMDA archives fiscal-year new-drug lists. The Japanese approval table explicitly identifies 新有効成分含有医薬品 (new active ingredient). MHLW issues dated NHI price-list amendments with product code, ingredient, strength and effective date. Use the first listing only under a separately named endpoint. A complete, openly downloadable series of original generic **approval decisions** with day-level dates was unverified. Match Japanese Accepted Names to INNs and GSRS, retain salts and combinations, and reconcile era dates. [PMDA archive](https://www.pmda.go.jp/review-services/drug-reviews/review-information/p-drugs/0010.html) · [September 2026 approval table](https://www.pmda.go.jp/files/000281577.pdf) · [June 2025 NHI amendment and annexes](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/web/t_doc?dataId=00tc9492&dataType=1&pageNo=1)

PMDA review reports can document the actual re-examination term for a specific indication. The December 2020 rivaroxaban decision specifies four years. JPO provides patent records and a limited API. A dated, openly licensed product-to-patent crosswalk and complete historical amendments remain unverified. Prefer official downloadable documents. MHLW applies PDL 1.0 by default, with attribution and exceptions. The licence says “Commercial use of ‘This Content’ is also permitted.” PMDA permits reuse of its own material subject to exceptions, including manufacturer-owned application summaries. Bulk crawling permission was unverified. **China.** NMPA distinguishes Class 1 innovative chemical drugs and overseas-marketed originators. A Class 1-only roster would omit some locally new ingredients. The domestic/imported databases and dated certificate-delivery notices give product and registration identifiers. Verify the original decision date independently of certificate issue, renewal or dispatch. Map Chinese generic names, occasional English names and salts to INN/UNII with bilingual adjudication. Keep first-generic review recommendations and consistency evaluations separate from final marketing authorization. [NMPA product fields](https://www.nmpa.gov.cn/qtfwzy/qtfwzyfw/qtfwzywfjsphead/20200628171201427.html) · [Delivery notices](https://www.nmpa.gov.cn/directory/web/nmpa/zwfw/zwfwpjfbzs/pjfbzsyp/index.html) · [Registration categories](https://english.nmpa.gov.cn/2026-10/27/c_1226985.htm)

China's patent-linkage platform dates from 2021. The 2026 data-protection work procedure, arts. 6 and 11, calls for publication of awards and updates on termination. An open bulk archive of drug-linked patents, expiry dates, protection decisions and publication history was unverified. The English site says “All rights reserved.” The Chinese website statement requires authorization for material supplied by other parties, and a direct request for that statement returned HTTP 412. No open grant or documented automated export for the required regulatory tables was established. **Blocked for open benchmark ingestion.** [CDE platform](https://zldj.cde.org.cn/home) · [CNIPA history](https://english.cnipa.gov.cn/module/download/down.jsp?colID=2936&i_ID=176472) · [NMPA procedure and footer](https://english.nmpa.gov.cn/2026-10/30/c_1227044.htm) · [Website statement](https://www.nmpa.gov.cn/hudong/wzhshm/index.html)

**Singapore.** The English HSA register on data.gov.sg has a downloadable CSV and API, SIN product number, approval date, ATC code and ingredient text. Its 12-column schema lacks application class. Separate HSA decision lists distinguish NDA-1 (first strength of a locally new entity, filter biologics) and GDAs. GDA-1 means the applicant's first strength. The 2024 PDF lists pregabalin GDA-1 and GDA-2 on the same day. Join by SIN, then take the earliest generic across applicants and strengths after an eligible originator. Annual GDA PDFs for 2024 and 2025 and monthly 2026 rows exist. Earlier completeness, withdrawals and archived release times remain unknown. [Register, 7 August 2026 edition](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_767279312753558cbf19d48344577084/view) · [Application definitions](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/therapeutic-products/registration-of-therapeutic-products/register-your-therapeutic-product/registration-of-therapeutic-products/application-types/) · [Decision archive](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/therapeutic-products/registration-of-therapeutic-products/tools-and-resources/listing-of-approvals-and-post-registration-actions/listing-of-approvals/)

The Singapore Open Data Licence v1.0 permits “download, copy, distribute, transmit, modify and adapt” with attribution. HSA website terms §4.3 require “written permission from the Health Sciences Authority” for reproduction beyond Copyright Act permissions. A redistributable application-class feed was unverified. Regulation 23 requires a patent declaration. Regulations 26 and 29 identify distinct protection clocks. No openly downloadable, dated ingredient-to-declaration/protection register verified. **Open register, blocked historical endpoint join.** [Data licence](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence) · [HSA terms](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/terms-of-use/) · [Declaration guidance](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/therapeutic-products/guidance-documents/) · [Singapore regulations](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/HPA2007-S329-2016)

**Malaysia.** NPRA supplies daily English administrative records as CSV/Parquet under “Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)”. They include MAL registration number, registration and end dates, ingredient and MDC code. The 27 November 2026 preview already has future-dated registrations. The 8 June 2026 DCA meeting lists new approvals and renewals separately. Row 250 labels a November 2026 date as re-registration. Row 29 calls a new combination an NCE subcategory. Use original decisions and precise local categories. Prescription status and a later registration date alone cannot identify a generic. Join Malay/English meeting records by MAL and normalize ingredient strings from the open register. [NPRA open data, dictionary and terms](https://data.moh.gov.my/data-catalogue/pharmaceutical_products) · [DCA meeting 421, rows 29–31 and 250](https://www.npra.gov.my/easyarticles/images/users/1153/DCA/New%20Product%20Approved/2026/Senarai-produk-yang-diluluskan-dalam-PBKD-421-8-Jun-2026-website.pdf)

Machine-readable drug-to-patent links, historical daily snapshots, permission to redistribute meeting/DE PDFs and automated patent access were unverified. **Open inventory, historical endpoint and predictor rights incomplete.** [NPRA registers](https://www.npra.gov.my/~npraweb/index.php/en/data-exclusivity.html) · [April 2026 DE table, row 1](https://www.npra.gov.my/~npraweb/images/2019/12/NCE/2_DE_Table_Update_10-04-2026.pdf) · [MyIPO](https://www.myipo.gov.my/patent-search/)

**Thailand.** Two routes supplement the earlier EVP access failures. Thai/English names and TMT identifiers need mappings at ingredient level, with calendar conversion. The 2007 trade-secret regulation describes confidentiality. Product-specific data-protection dates and an open drug-linked patent history remain unverified. ## Decision and development-only feasibility card

**Power priority, subject to the open-data rule.** 1 Canada for its closest approval analogue and open decision/patent extracts. 2 Japan for its dated open publications, with reimbursement as a separate endpoint until original generic approvals can be reconstructed. 3 Singapore for its explicit application types. 4 Malaysia for its open inventory and dated decisions. 5 China for high potential volume conditional on open record-level rights and final-decision linkage. 6 Thailand for unresolved roster and label coverage. These are priorities for obtaining usable independent events. Actual comparative power is unknown.

**Thai-context priority.** 1 Thailand for national registration and patent questions. 2 Singapore for a regional approval comparator with explicit generic classifications. 3 Malaysia for reusable ingredient inventory and a publicly inspectable local exclusivity register. 4 Japan for a regional reimbursement comparison. 5 China for patent-linkage context. 6 Canada for a methodological control. Only Thai records can establish Thai status. Lexchin's Canadian cohort demonstrates a paired originator/generic NOC workflow. The named comparator for country selection is the registered US LOE-Bench endpoint. No head-to-head evaluation was run. [Primary cohort study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28964446/) · [US benchmark](PREREGISTRATION.md)

1. Before inspecting counts, register the countries, endpoint and years. Start with Canada and Japan. Select three consecutive, complete development years before the prospective freeze and enough prior history to identify an earlier generic. Declare exclusions for biologics, supplements, administrative actions, authorized generics, conditional decisions and incomplete lookback. Preserve same-day ties. Keep actual approval, publication, listing and first sale as separate dates.
2. For each source, capture the entitled file/API sample, issuer, licence version and exceptions, retrieval UTC, original edition and publication witness, exact row/page and SHA-256. Attempt anonymous restoration. Seek dated, pre-origin editions for roster and features, including changes and removals. Apply the preregistered freshness/availability rules. A newly retrieved historical decision can adjudicate an outcome after maturity; retrospective corrections cannot silently enter forecasts.
3. Hand-check stratified samples of original, generic, duplicate strength, salt, combination, supplement/renewal and withdrawn products against the dated decisions. Map local product IDs to their full ingredient sets, then use versioned GSRS UNII and active-moiety relationships. Report exact, adjudicated, ambiguous and missing mappings and uncovered years. Preserve unresolved cases as unknown. [GSRS](https://gsrs.ncats.nih.gov/)
4. Count unique eligible first decisions **within each country and year**, with candidate rows, original-approval matches, firsts lost to earlier decisions, ambiguous dates and final denominator. Report overlap with US moiety sets, distinct added drugs, annual queue size and usable patent/protection coverage. Simulate the local 20% queue using shared-moiety clusters and repeated origins. Compare marginal precision and power for M3 versus M1 under the same predeclared scenarios. Do not use protected outcomes for this choice. Advance an approval cohort only after open restoration, as-of replay and hand-checked endpoint reconstruction pass. Keep a listing-only result descriptive or separately registered.
