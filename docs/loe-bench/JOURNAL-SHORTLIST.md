# Journal shortlist for the first LOE-Bench paper

Checked 28 November 2026 ICT. Start with **Pharmaceutical Sciences Asia** for a fee-free pharmaceutical sciences audience. **Medical Decision Making** is the strongest verified international option for a paper centered on the one-in-five decision. **Therapeutic Innovation & Regulatory Science** is a close editorial fit with a zero-APC subscription route. Its other charges need verification before it enters the queue.

The paper evaluates a preregistered historical benchmark for the first **final US generic approval** using FDA evidence available at each cutoff. At a 20% review budget and 24 months, the graph-augmented queue captured 66.85% of eventual approval cases at cutoff versus 46.48% for drug age (H1 +20.37 percentage points, 95% CI +14.37 to +26.16, PASS). Its gain over the dated-evidence table was +1.36 points (95% CI −2.20 to +3.01, H2 FAIL). H3 is descriptive, the LLM comparison exploratory, and the auto-clear check failed. The Thai-context dossier illustrates specialist hand-off. The outcome is FDA approval. Launch, Thai transfer and legal clearance require separate evidence. Hu et al. forecast first ANDA **submissions**, a different target, using ML. LOE-Bench's measured comparator for H1 is the drug-age queue B1. [Publication plan](ENDGAME-PLAN.md#publication) · [IS2](../writing/is2/mwd/IS2-DRAFT-EN.md#abstract) · [SCORE](receipts/SCORE.md#primary-family) · [Hu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpt.1479).

## How to read the venue evidence

- **FREE** means an official journal or society statement supports a zero-cost route for a standard article. Use the standard license where indicated and omit elective print colour, reprints and other paid extras. **CONDITIONAL** means the publisher confirms a subscription option with no APC or no ordinary publishing charge, but title-level evidence has a gap in mandatory fees. **NOT FREE** means an author fee applies to the ordinary research route. **UNVERIFIED** identifies missing, inaccessible or ambiguous evidence.
- In the international listings, a linked current SCImago record is evidence of Scopus-derived coverage. The quartile is the 2025 SJR for the named category. Standalone WoS collection labels require independent evidence. A JIF alone leaves the collection UNVERIFIED. Time means submission to first decision or to publication as labeled, and an editorial target is identified as such.
- Public [OSF registration P2SMD](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/P2SMD) records the benchmark protocol. The latest fetched lane handoff records the completed Git freeze on 28 November at 11:18:44 UTC and the audited OSF Update 4 sheet awaiting owner filing. The remote tag was verified. The public OSF timestamp of the complete prediction package remains UNVERIFIED. [Track A lane handoff](https://github.com/siriponsri/patent-to-pilot/blob/ef3718173c6e61ce3a2ddcca2e3c4289bec3bbdf/docs/loe-bench/HANDOFF.md#track-a-frozen-28-nov-2026-111844-utc). Inventory where the TH and EN IS2 short papers were deposited, whether either appeared in proceedings, and whether a license applies.
