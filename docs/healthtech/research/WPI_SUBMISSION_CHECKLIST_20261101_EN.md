## Journal fit

- The research question is centered on patent information, medical-device patent evidence, provenance, retrieval, and decision support.
- The paper does not present a generic web RAG demo as the contribution.
- The contribution is bounded by documented relationships, incomplete judgments, and reproducible evidence acquisition.
- Clinical, legal, FTO, patent-validity, investment, and market claims are explicitly out of scope.

## Scientific integrity

- Freeze the final cohort, group split, cutoffs, primary endpoint, K, resource budget, and retry rules before the final run.
- Bind every figure/table to one candidate revision, one protocol version, and one audit manifest.
- Keep `disclosed_link`, `retrieved_candidate`, and `unjudged` distinct.
- Do not use an agent judge or similarity score as semantic gold.
- Report failures, retries, blocked branches, missing judgments, denominators, and group counts.
- Keep local/API model-size and provider confounds visible.

## Required manuscript sections to confirm against the live guide

- title, abstract, keywords, introduction, related work, methods, results, discussion, limitations, conclusion;
- data/code/materials availability and rights statement;
- ethics statement, funding, conflicts, author contributions, and AI-use disclosure;
- figure/table captions and machine-readable source tables where requested;
- reference style, word limits, supplementary files, highlights/graphical abstract requirements, and file formats.
