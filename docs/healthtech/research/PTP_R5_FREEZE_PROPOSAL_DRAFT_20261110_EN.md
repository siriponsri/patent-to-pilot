## Execution boundary and report

After all gates pass, present the complete concrete manifest and panel for owner
approval. Until then, no test preparation that exposes protected material is authorized.
The current instruction forbids --split test. Any required runtime permission change
must be explicitly included in the owner-approved action, not inferred from this plan.

Selected rankings must correspond to the approved immutable code/configuration and
be generated without qrels in the approved environment. The protected evaluator then
produces one complete report for all declared systems/contrasts, including negative
outcomes, uncertainty, failures and costs. No released score may trigger a new prompt,
depth, view, method nomination or tuning on the same protected set.

Infrastructure failure preserves attempted outputs and access/exposure logs. It does
not authorize method changes or an automatic repeat. Assess what performance data
were exposed and obtain the necessary reviewed recovery decision. A scientific failure
is an outcome, not an infrastructure exception.
