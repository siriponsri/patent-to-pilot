Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “Evaluating Adaptive Evidence Acquisition for Medical-Device Patent Screening under Incomplete Judgments,” for publication in *World Patent Information*.

The manuscript studies a patent-information problem at the intersection of medical-device evidence, retrieval, and reproducible decision support. We compare fixed and adaptive evidence-acquisition policies under shared evidence access, incomplete documented links, explicit unknowns, group-aware leakage controls, and resource accounting. The paper also provides a provenance-aware dossier workflow that preserves source locators, candidate/disclosure state, history, and exportability.

The manuscript is intentionally bounded. It reports descriptive computational evidence and real permitted case studies; it does not claim semantic patent relevance gold, exhaustive recall, clinical suitability, FTO, patent validity, investment merit, independent user validation, revenue, willingness-to-pay, or ROI. The current project is a solo computational study, and no external participants or participant data were used.
